How to send a parcel at a J&T drop point

  1. Pack and seal the parcel. Use a box or thick pouch; the counter staff weigh and measure it.
  2. Have the recipient's details ready: full name, phone number and address with postcode.
  3. Create the waybill. Fill it in at the counter, or show the booking if you created the shipment in the J&T app beforehand.
  4. Pay and keep the receipt. The waybill number on it is your tracking number.

Dropping off Shopee, Lazada and TikTok Shop orders

Sellers whose orders ship with J&T print the platform's J&T label and hand the parcel in at a J&T drop point; no counter waybill is needed. Check that the label shows J&T Express before you go, because platforms also use other couriers.

J&T drop point opening hours

Hours are set by each drop point. Many open every day, including weekends, from about 9am to 8pm, but some close earlier or on public holidays. The Google Maps listing shows each outlet's hours and the Open now filter hides closed ones.

Before you go: parcel sealed, recipient name, phone and full address with postcode, and payment method. For marketplace orders, the printed J&T label.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the nearest J&T drop point?

Tap Use my location above to open Google Maps showing J&T Express drop points around you, or type your area or postcode. Each listing shows the address, opening hours and directions.

Is J&T open on Sunday?

Many J&T drop points open on weekends, including Sunday, but hours are set by each outlet. Check the outlet's hours on Google Maps or use the Open now filter before you go.

Can I drop off a Shopee parcel at any J&T drop point?

If the parcel has a J&T Express label from Shopee, you can usually hand it in at a J&T drop point. Check the courier name on the label first, because Shopee also ships with other couriers.

What do I need to send a parcel at J&T?

Bring the sealed parcel, the recipient's name, phone number and full address with postcode, and a way to pay. Keep the receipt; its waybill number is your tracking number.
