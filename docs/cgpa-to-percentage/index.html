CGPA to Percentage Calculator

To convert CGPA to percentage, multiply your CGPA by 9.5 unless your university publishes its own formula. A CGPA of 8.2 is 77.9% this way. VTU, AKTU, MAKAUT and SPPU engineering use (CGPA − 0.75) × 10 (74.5%), and Anna University uses CGPA × 10 (82%). Pick your university below to see your exact figure.

Things to know

  • Your university's conversion certificate is the official figure. Request one from the examination section if a form asks for a percentage.
  • SGPA (one semester) converts with the same formula as CGPA at most universities.
  • Results below 0% are shown as 0%, and above 100% as 100%.
  • For a grading scale other than 10, choose "Any scale" or enter your university's formula as a custom formula.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert CGPA to percentage?

Multiply your CGPA by 9.5 if your university does not publish its own formula. This is the CBSE method and the most widely used default, so a CGPA of 8.2 equals 77.9%. If your university publishes a formula, use that instead: for example VTU, AKTU, MAKAUT and SPPU engineering use (CGPA − 0.75) × 10, which gives 74.5% for 8.2.

What is 8 CGPA in percentage?

8 CGPA is 76% with the CBSE formula (8 × 9.5), 80% with CGPA × 10, 72.5% with (CGPA − 0.75) × 10, 75% with (CGPA − 0.5) × 10 and 67.8% with the University of Mumbai formula (8 × 7.1 + 11).

Why does CBSE multiply CGPA by 9.5?

CBSE derived the factor from past results: students in the top grade band scored about 95% on average, so a 10-point CGPA is scaled by 9.5 to approximate a percentage. Many institutions also use CGPA × 9.5 when a university has not published its own conversion.

Which formula should I use for a job or admission form?

Use the formula printed on your marksheet or in your university's conversion certificate. If you do not have one, ask your university's examination section for a CGPA-to-percentage conversion certificate, because employers and admission offices may accept only the official figure.

How do I convert a 4.0 GPA to percentage?

There is no single standard. A simple proportional conversion is GPA ÷ 4 × 100, so 3.5 becomes 87.5%. Many universities and foreign credential evaluators use their own tables, so check the receiving institution's requirement.

How do I convert percentage to CGPA?

Reverse the same formula. With CGPA × 9.5, divide the percentage by 9.5: 75% is about 7.89 CGPA. With (CGPA − 0.75) × 10, divide by 10 and add 0.75: 75% is 8.25 CGPA. The converter does this for whichever formula you choose.

Does this converter store my results?

No. It runs entirely in your browser and works offline after your first visit. A copied link keeps your inputs after the # sign, which is not sent to any server.
