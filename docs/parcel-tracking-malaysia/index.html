      How to track a parcel in Malaysia

      1. Find the tracking number. It is on your order page (Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop), in the seller's message or SMS, or printed above the barcode on the consignment note.
      2. Paste it above and press Track. Spaces and dashes are removed automatically.
      3. Open the courier's page. The number is copied when you tap the link, so you can paste it if the page opens empty.
      4. Not recognised? Pick the courier from the list, or use 17TRACK, which checks over a thousand couriers at once.

      Parcel not moving? What to do

      • No update for 24 hours after the label was made: normal; the first scan happens at pick-up.
      • Stuck at one hub for 3 or more working days: contact the courier through its official website or app with the tracking number.
      • Marketplace orders: raise it in the Shopee, Lazada or TikTok Shop order page before the order's protection period ends; the platform can chase the courier for you.
      • Marked delivered but not received: report it to the seller and courier the same day; many couriers keep a delivery photo or signature.

      Frequently asked questions

      How do I track my parcel in Malaysia?

      Paste the tracking number into the box above and press Track. The tool reads the number's format, shows the matching courier and opens that courier's official tracking page with the number already copied, so you can paste it if the page asks. If you already know the courier, pick it from the courier list instead.

      Which courier is my tracking number from?

      Look at the start of the number. Two or three letters, 9 digits and MY at the end (such as ER123456789MY) is Pos Laju. SPXMY, or MY followed by 12 or 13 digits and a letter, is SPX Express (Shopee). NV, NVMY or MYNJV is Ninja Van. 1Z is UPS. A plain 12-digit number is usually J&T Express. The courier name is also printed on the consignment note and shown on your order page.

      How do I track a Shopee, Lazada or TikTok Shop parcel?

      Open the order in the app and note the courier and tracking number under shipping information. Shopee mostly ships with SPX Express, Lazada with Lazada Logistics (LEX), and TikTok Shop with partners such as J&T Express and Ninja Van. Paste the number here to open that courier's own tracking page.

      Why does my tracking number show no result?

      A new label often shows nothing for up to 24 hours, until the courier scans the parcel at pick-up or drop-off. Check that you used the right courier and copied the number exactly, without spaces. If nothing appears after 2 working days, ask the seller to confirm the number and courier.

      How long does delivery take in Malaysia?

      Within Peninsular Malaysia most standard parcels arrive in 1 to 3 working days. Between Peninsular and Sabah, Sarawak or Labuan allow 3 to 7 working days. Rural areas, public holidays, sale seasons and bad weather add delays; the courier's tracking page gives the current estimate.

      What does Return to sender mean?

      The courier is sending the parcel back to the seller, usually after failed delivery attempts, a wrong or incomplete address, or a refused parcel. Contact the seller quickly; some couriers can redeliver if you call before the return is processed.

      Is this parcel tracker free and private?

      Yes. It is free with no signup. Detection runs in your browser, the number is never sent to this site, and recent numbers are stored only on your device. Delivery status comes from the courier's own website, or from 17TRACK only if you choose Show status on this page.
