    How working days are counted

    1. Calendar days from the start date to the end date, both included.
    2. Minus weekends: Friday and Saturday in Kedah, Kelantan and Terengganu; Saturday and Sunday elsewhere.
    3. Minus public holidays on workdays, federal and state, with replacement days.
    4. Minus annual leave you plan to take.
    5. Deadline: step forward from the start date, counting only working days.

    Public holiday rules

    • Weekends: Kedah, Kelantan and Terengganu rest on Friday and Saturday; Johor moved to Saturday and Sunday on 1 January 2025.
    • Extra Hari Raya holiday 2026: Friday 20 March 2026 nationwide, plus Thursday 19 March in Kelantan.
    • Islamic dates follow the official moon sighting and can shift by a day.
    • Employees: the Employment Act 1955 gives 11 paid public holidays a year, five of them fixed; employers pick the rest from gazetted holidays.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many working days are there in 2027 in Malaysia?

    It depends on the state. Selangor has 248 working days in 2027, Kuala Lumpur 247, Johor 248, Penang 247, Sabah 246 and Sarawak 247. Fri–Sat states have fewer: Kedah 244, Kelantan 241 and Terengganu 239.

    How many working days are in 2026?

    Selangor has 244 working days in 2026. For the rest of the year, November 2026 has 20 working days in Selangor and December 2026 has 21.

    Which states have a Friday–Saturday weekend?

    Kedah, Kelantan and Terengganu. Johor returned to a Saturday–Sunday weekend on 1 January 2025, so every other state and federal territory rests on Saturday and Sunday.

    What happens when a public holiday falls on a weekend?

    In most cases the next working day is a replacement holiday. In Selangor, Deepavali on Sunday 8 November 2026 is replaced on Monday 9 November. Saturday–Sunday states usually replace only Sunday holidays.

    What date is 14 working days from today?

    Count only working days, skipping weekends and public holidays. 14 working days from Wednesday 14 October 2026 in Selangor ends on 3 November 2026: three weekends (six days) are skipped.

    Are the Islamic holiday dates final?

    Hari Raya Aidilfitri, Aidiladha, Awal Muharram, Maulidur Rasul, Nuzul al-Quran and Israk Mikraj follow the official moon sighting, so a date can move by a day. Ad hoc holidays declared later are not included until they are announced.

    How many public holidays does Selangor have in 2027?

    20 public holiday dates, including replacement days. Some fall on weekends, so they do not reduce the working day count.
