How the rate is worked out

  1. Profit needed so that profit − EPF − SOCSO − tax = take-home × 12.
  2. Tax on chargeable income = profit − RM9,000 − EPF (up to RM4,000) − other reliefs, at resident rates, less the RM400 rebate up to RM35,000.
  3. Revenue = profit + business expenses × 12.
  4. Billable hours = hours a week × (52 − weeks off).
  5. Hourly rate = revenue ÷ billable hours; day rate = hourly × 8.

Tax and savings for freelancers

  • Form B: freelance and other business income is declared on Form B by 30 June; keep receipts for expenses.
  • EPF i-Saraan: voluntary savings for the self-employed; EPF relief is up to RM4,000 a year.
  • SOCSO SKSPS: required for self-employed workers in covered sectors.
  • Budget 2027: proposes RM12,000 self relief and lower rates from YA2027; compare them in the income tax calculator.
  • SST: only if your taxable services pass the registration threshold.
