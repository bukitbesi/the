Iddah and Ihdad Calculator

The iddah after a husband's death is 4 Hijri months and 10 days (about 130 days), with ihdad. After a divorce, a woman who still menstruates waits three periods of purity, ending when the third menstruation after the talak begins (at least 32 days); no menstruation: 3 Hijri months; pregnant: until delivery; before consummation: no iddah. How this calculator works

  1. Three periods: the next periods are projected from the start of your last period and your cycle. The iddah ends at the start of the third period after the talak. If the talak was during purity, at least 32 days; during menstruation, at least 47.
  2. Three months: the same Hijri date three months later.
  3. Four months and ten days: the same Hijri date four months later, plus ten days.
  4. Hijri months follow Malaysia's official takwim (MABIMS imkanur rukyah criteria), so the number of days depends on whether months have 29 or 30 days.

Ihdad: what a widow avoids

JAKIM's guideline makes ihdad (mourning) required of a woman whose husband has died. Throughout the iddah she avoids:

  • perfume and scented products;
  • kohl, make-up and henna;
  • jewellery and ornaments;
  • bright or decorative clothing worn to adorn.

She stays in the home she lived in when her husband died, and may go out for needs such as work, medical care or buying necessities.

Rights during the iddah

  • Talak raj'i: the wife is entitled to maintenance and housing throughout the iddah, and the husband may reconcile before it ends.
  • Iddah maintenance and mut'ah can be claimed in your state's Syariah Court.
  • Remarriage: a woman may not marry another man until the iddah ends.
