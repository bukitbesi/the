How the education cost is worked out

  1. Each year's cost = today's yearly cost × (1 + inflation)^(years until that year).
  2. Needed on day one = each year's cost discounted at the savings return to the start of university.
  3. Existing savings grow at the savings return until then.
  4. Monthly saving = shortfall ÷ the future value of saving RM1 at the start of each month.

Saving for education in Malaysia

  • Simpan SSPN: 4.10% dividend for 2025 and up to RM8,000 tax relief a year until YA2027.
  • Start early: each year of delay raises the monthly amount sharply.
  • Review yearly: update costs, inflation and your savings as your child grows.
