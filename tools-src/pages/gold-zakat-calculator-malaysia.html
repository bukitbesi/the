How to calculate gold and savings zakat

  1. Separate your gold: kept gold (investment) and worn gold (jewellery you wear).
  2. Kept gold: at 85 g or more held for a year, zakat is 2.5% of the full value.
  3. Worn gold: deduct your state's uruf; zakat is 2.5% on the weight above it only.
  4. Savings: if the balance stays at or above the nisab for the full year, zakat is 2.5%.
  5. Pay and claim the rebate: pay your state zakat body and keep the receipt for the tax rebate.
