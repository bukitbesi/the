How the rates are worked out

  1. BNM rate: the Kuala Lumpur interbank middle rate at 12:00, in ringgit per 1 or 100 units.
  2. Per unit = rate ÷ unit; the ringgit = 1.
  3. Conversion = amount × (ringgit per unit of the source currency) ÷ (ringgit per unit of the target).
  4. Money changer gap = (offered rate − BNM rate) ÷ BNM rate.

Tips for changing money

  • Compare several BNM-licensed money changers, because margins differ between counters.
  • Debit and credit cards abroad use the card network's rate plus bank fees; check your bank's terms.
  • Hajj and umrah: the Saudi riyal is listed; plan your savings with the hajj cost calculator.
  • See also the gold price today, which uses BNM's USD/MYR rate.
