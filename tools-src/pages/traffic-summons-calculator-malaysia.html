Traffic Summons Calculator Malaysia 2026

From 1 January 2026, a RM300 JPJ or PDRM summons costs RM150 if paid within 15 days, RM200 from day 16 to 30, and RM300 from day 31 to 60; after 60 days there is no compound and the case can go to court. Enter the notice date and compound to see what you pay now and the last day of each rate.

How the compound is worked out

  1. Days are counted from the notice date (day 1).
  2. Days 1–15: compound × 50%.
  3. Days 16–30: compound × ⅔ (RM200 on RM300).
  4. Days 31–60: the full compound; day 61+: no compound.

Key points

  • JPJ, PDRM and AwAS use the same rates from 1 January 2026; there are no more discount campaigns.
  • Non-compoundable offences (no insurance, no road tax, no licence, racing) go straight to court.
  • Summonses from before 2026: the tiered rates do not apply; check the amount in MyJPJ or MyBayar PDRM.
  • From 1 January 2029 the maximum compound rises to RM500 (Road Transport (Amendment) Act 2026).
