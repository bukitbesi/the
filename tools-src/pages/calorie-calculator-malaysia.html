How calories are worked out

  1. BMR (Mifflin-St Jeor) = 10 × weight + 6.25 × height − 5 × age + 5 (men) or − 161 (women).
  2. Daily need = BMR × activity factor (1.2 to 1.9).
  3. Target = daily need ± (kg a week × 7,700 ÷ 7).
  4. Macronutrients: 55% carbohydrate and 15% protein ÷ 4 kcal a gram, 30% fat ÷ 9 kcal a gram.

Malaysian healthy eating guidance

  • Healthy Plate (Suku-Suku Separuh): a quarter grains, a quarter protein, half vegetables and fruit.
  • Asian BMI: normal is 18.5 to 22.9; a waist over 90 cm (men) or 80 cm (women) signals risk.
  • Gradual weight loss is easier to keep; ask your doctor if you have a chronic illness.
  • Check your BMI with the BMI calculator.
