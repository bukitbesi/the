Faraid Calculator Malaysia

Faraid divides a Muslim estate after funeral costs, debts, harta sepencarian and any bequest (at most 1/3): husband 1/2 or 1/4, wife 1/4 or 1/8, mother 1/3 or 1/6, daughters 1/2 or 2/3, and the rest to residuaries such as sons, who take twice a daughter's share. In Malaysia a residue with no residuary goes to Baitulmal. Enter the estate and heirs below.

How faraid is worked out

  1. Deduct funeral costs, debts, harta sepencarian and any bequest to non-heirs (at most 1/3 of what is left).
  2. Find excluded heirs (mahjub), for example grandchildren by a son and siblings by the father.
  3. Give the fixed shares (ashab al-furud); if they exceed the estate, raise the base ('awl).
  4. Give the residue to the residuaries in order; if there are none, to Baitulmal (or radd in Perlis).

The estate process in Malaysia

  • Sijil Faraid is issued by the state Syariah High Court on the death certificate and the heirs' identity cards.
  • Small estates (up to RM5 million with no civil will): the JKPTG estate distribution unit, through MyLand.
  • Summary estates (movable property only, up to RM600,000): Amanah Raya Berhad.
  • Larger estates or with a will: the civil High Court (probate or letters of administration).
  • Hibah given in life and EPF money paid to a nominee are outside faraid. Work out zakat with the zakat calculator.
