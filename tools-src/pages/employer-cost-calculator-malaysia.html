How employer cost is worked out

  1. Wages = basic salary + fixed allowances (wages for EPF and SOCSO).
  2. Employer EPF = 13% (wages up to RM5,000) or 12%, on the Third Schedule wage band, rounded up.
  3. SOCSO and EIS by the banded contribution schedules in RM100 steps up to the RM6,000 ceiling.
  4. HRD Corp levy = 1% or 0.5% of Malaysian employees' wages.
  5. Yearly = monthly × 12 + bonus + EPF on the bonus.
