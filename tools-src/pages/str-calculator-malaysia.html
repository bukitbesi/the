How STR and SARA are worked out

  1. Category: household, single aged 21 to 59, or senior citizen 60+ without a spouse.
  2. Income band: up to RM2,500 or RM2,501 to RM5,000 (singles up to RM2,500 only).
  3. Children: 0, 1 to 2, 3 to 4, or 5 and more sets the household STR rate.
  4. SARA: RM1,200 a year for households (RM2,400 eKasih); RM600 for singles.

Eligibility and how to apply

  • Malaysian citizens only. Households: a married couple or single parent with gross household income of RM5,000 or less.
  • Singles: aged 21 to 59 (OKU 19 to 59 with a JKM OKU card), income RM2,500 or less, not a full-time student.
  • Senior citizens without a spouse: 60 or older; sources state an income cap of RM2,500 or RM5,000.
  • Apply or update at bantuantunai.hasil.gov.my (MySTR) or with form BK-01; check status and appeal on the same portal.
  • SARA is credited to MyKad automatically; there is no separate application.
