    PDF page sizes

    {{PDF_SIZE_ROWS}}
    Page sizes offered for Images to PDF
    SizeMillimetresPDF points

    PDF measures pages in points, 72 to the inch. "Same as image" makes each page fit its picture at 96 pixels per inch, the screen standard, so a 1080 px photo becomes a 810-point (285.75 mm) page.

    Honest limits

    • PDF to Word rebuilds text, paragraphs, headings and lists. It does not rebuild tables, columns, images or exact fonts, and scanned PDFs need OCR.
    • Word to PDF keeps headings, lists, tables, images and basic formatting, but simplifies headers, footers, text boxes and page numbers.
    • Compress helps scanned and photo-heavy PDFs; text PDFs are usually already small.
    • Password-protected PDFs can be read and converted to images or Word after you enter the password, but merging and splitting need an unprotected copy.
