Marriage ages differ by law: non-Muslims 18 (girls 16 or 17 with the Chief Minister's or Menteri Besar's licence); Muslims 18 for men and 16 for women, younger only with Syariah Court consent, except Selangor and Kedah, which set 18 for women.

How age is worked out

  1. Years and months: full months since the date of birth; if the day of birth does not exist in a month (say the 31st), the month's last day is used.
  2. Days: the days left after the last full month.
  3. 29 February: the birthday falls on 1 March in common years.
  4. Hijri age: the date of birth and today are converted to Malaysia's Hijri takwim, then the Hijri years and months are counted.

Other things to know

  • MyKad: applying more than 30 days after the 12th birthday costs RM10 until age 16, and counts as late after that.
  • Undi18: the Election Commission gazettes the automatic voter rolls every month.
  • Child workers: under 15 is a child and 15 to 17 a young person; hazardous work is banned under 18.
  • Work out your retirement date and pension with the retirement age calculator, and convert dates with the Hijri date converter.
