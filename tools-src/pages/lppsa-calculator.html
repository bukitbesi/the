LPPSA's formula: rate = (A₁ × 4% + A₂ × 4.5%) ÷ amount, where A₁ is the part up to RM750,000 and A₂ the part above it, counted across first and second financing. Joint applicants (husband and wife) are worked out separately.

How it is calculated

  1. Net income = basic salary + fixed allowances − mandatory deductions.
  2. Table limit comes from the net income table above (for a second financing, minus the first amount).
  3. Highest instalment is the lowest of: 60% × (basic + fixed allowances); 80% × net income − other loans; and current take-home pay − 20% × gross income.
  4. Tenure = 420 months (SPPM 480, second 360), capped at age 90 (pension) or retirement age (EPF).
  5. Maximum financing = the lowest of the table limit, the amount the highest instalment can repay, and the property price (90% for a second financing while the first is active).

LPPSA's payslip example: basic RM3,371, ITP RM400, ITKA RM250, COLA RM300 (RM4,321 in all), tax RM21, personal loan and Angkasa RM772, zakat and Tabung Haji RM63. Net income RM4,300, table limit {{L_EX_MAX}}, highest instalment {{L_EX_ROOM}}, and RM550,000 over 35 years is {{L_EX_PAY}} a month.

LPPSA rules to know

  • SPPM (age 30 and under): first financing only, Types 1, 2, 3, 5 (house) and 6, tenure up to 40 years; open until 31 December 2026 (subject to extension).
  • Second financing: 100% margin if the first is settled, 90% while it is active, 100% for Type 5 (takeover) and Type 7 (renovation) on the same property.
  • Instalment method: LPPSA charges profit on a reducing balance; this calculator uses monthly rests. LPPSA's SmartKira also includes the insurance/takaful interim period, so the official figure can differ slightly.
  • Second financing tenure is 360 months under LPPSA's tenure notice (Budget 2019); the 2026 notices do not change it.
