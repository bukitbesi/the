How annual leave is worked out

  1. Entitlement by service: 8 days (under 2 years), 12 days (2 to under 5) or 16 days (5 or more).
  2. Pro rata in a part year: entitlement × completed months ÷ 12.
  3. Rounding: under half a day is dropped; half a day or more counts as a full day.
  4. Unused leave on leaving × ordinary rate of pay (monthly wages ÷ 26).

Leave rules in the Employment Act

  • Coverage: all employees from 1 January 2023, regardless of wages.
  • Hospitalisation: 60 days a year, separate from ordinary sick leave since the 2022 amendment.
  • Maternity: 98 consecutive days; paternity: 7 consecutive days for married fathers after 12 months' service, up to five confinements.
  • Your contract can give more leave, never less than the Act.
