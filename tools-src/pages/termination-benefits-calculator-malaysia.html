How termination benefits are worked out

  1. Service from the date you joined to your last day, with a part year to the nearest month.
  2. Days a year: 10 (under 2 years), 15 (2 to under 5) or 20 (5 or more).
  3. A day's wages = monthly wages ÷ 26 (ordinary rate of pay).
  4. Benefit = days a year × years of service × a day's wages.
  5. Notice: 4, 6 or 8 weeks (or your contract's longer period); pay in lieu ≈ monthly × 12 ÷ 52 per week.

Termination rules in Malaysia

  • Eligibility: at least 12 months' continuous service, ended by the employer (termination, retrenchment, lay-off or closure).
  • Not paid on resignation, on retirement at the agreed age, or on dismissal for misconduct after due inquiry.
  • RM4,000 threshold: from 1 January 2023 the Act covers all employees, but the minimum termination benefit is guaranteed only to those earning up to RM4,000 a month and to manual workers; others follow their contract.
  • Loss of job: PERKESO's Employment Insurance System may pay job search allowances after retrenchment; claim with PERKESO.
