Driving Licence Cost Malaysia 2026

JPJ fees for a new car licence are RM90: the learner's licence RM30 (3 months) and the probationary licence RM60 (2 years), excluding the driving school package. Renewing a car licence costs RM30 a year and a motorcycle RM20 a year, up to 10 years; a physical print is RM20. Pick your licence class.

Steps to a licence

  1. Register at a driving school and take a medical check.
  2. KPP01 course and the computer theory test.
  3. Learner's licence, then circuit (KPP02) and road (KPP03) training.
  4. JPJ tests Part II (circuit) and III (road), then the 2-year PDL and conversion to a CDL.

Things to know

  • The MyJPJ digital licence is free and can be renewed in the app.
  • A PDL is cancelled if KEJARA demerit points exceed the limit during probation; you must redo the course and tests.
  • B2-to-B upgrade is capped at RM160 including the JPJ test (from 1 October 2024).
  • Not confirmed: Part II and III test fees, school package ceilings and the 10-year renewal discount for 2026; check at the counter.
