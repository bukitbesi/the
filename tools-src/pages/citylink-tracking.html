City-Link Express Tracking

Enter your City-Link consignment number to open City-Link Express's official tracking page with the number copied. City-Link numbers are often 15 letters and digits. Below: what City-Link statuses mean and what to do if a delivery fails.

City-Link consignment number

  • City-Link calls the tracking number a consignment number, printed on the consignment note and label.
  • It is often 15 letters and digits. Because other couriers use similar lengths, the tracker does not guess City-Link from the format alone; on this page it opens City-Link directly.
  • Use the number exactly as printed, without spaces.

Common City-Link tracking statuses

StatusWhat it means
Shipment picked upCity-Link collected the parcel from the sender.
In transitThe parcel is moving between City-Link branches or hubs.
Arrived at delivery branchThe parcel reached the branch serving your address.
Out for deliveryA courier has the parcel for delivery.
DeliveredCity-Link recorded the delivery.
Delivery unsuccessful / ReturnedDelivery failed or the parcel is going back to the sender.

City-Link's exact wording may differ; the order of events is what matters.

If a City-Link delivery fails

  1. Check the tracking page for the reason and the branch holding the parcel.
  2. Contact City-Link through its official website with the consignment number to rearrange delivery or collect from the branch.
  3. For online orders, tell the seller as well.

About City-Link Express

City-Link Express was founded in 1979 by Dato' David Tan and is known as Malaysia's first locally owned courier company. It was incorporated in 1980 and is headquartered in Shah Alam, Selangor.
