How it is worked out

  1. Retirement date = your birthday at the retirement age (60 by default).
  2. Time left = years, months and days from today to that date.
  3. EPF ages = your birthdays at 50, 55, 60 and 75.
  4. Pension = 1/600 × months of service (up to 360) × final basic salary, at most 60%.
  5. Gratuity = 7.5% × months of service × final basic salary.

Retirement rules in Malaysia

  • Private sector: minimum retirement age 60 (Minimum Retirement Age Act 2012); working past 60 is by agreement.
  • Civil service: compulsory retirement at 60; optional from 40 with at least 10 years' service. JPA is studying 65.
  • EPF after 60: employer EPF 4%, employee 0% (voluntary); contributions mandatory to 75.
  • New civil servants: the Cabinet decided in January 2024 that new permanent posts will be non-pensionable, with EPF instead; existing officers keep their pension.
  • Leave cash-in (GCR): sources give different day limits, so it is left out; check with your HR or JPA.
