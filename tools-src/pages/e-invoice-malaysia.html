e-Invoice Malaysia Checker

From 1 September 2026, businesses with annual turnover below RM3 million are exempt from LHDN e-invoicing, unless a corporate shareholder, holding company, related company or joint venture has RM3 million or more. Larger businesses started in phases from 1 August 2024 to 1 January 2026, with no prosecution until 31 December 2027 for 2026 start dates. Answer four questions to see your date.

Your business

Does the business have a corporate shareholder, holding company, related company or joint venture with turnover of RM3 million or more?

Turnover means the revenue in your audited financial statements, or in your tax return if the accounts are not audited. Two companies owned by the same individuals are not related companies for this test.

How the exemption threshold changed

{{E_HISTORY}}
ThresholdWhen

The effective date of the RM3 million threshold is reported as 1 September 2026 (LHDN media release, announced in the Prime Minister's National Day address); the guideline itself was published on 30 August 2026.

Examples

  • Kedai runcit, RM400,000 in YA2022, sole proprietor: exempt. If revenue reaches RM3.1 million in YA2027, e-invoicing starts on 1 January 2029.
  • Subsidiary with RM400,000 revenue whose parent already issues e-invoices: not exempt, starts on 1 July 2026, relaxation until 31 December 2027.
  • Company with RM4 million in YA2022: Phase 4, started on 1 January 2026, no prosecution until 31 December 2027.

How the checker decides

  1. Phase: businesses operating in 2022 with YA2022 turnover of RM3 million or more fall into Phases 1 to 4 by turnover.
  2. Exemption: below RM3 million, the business is exempt unless the group-link test applies (section 1.6.10 of the e-Invoice Guideline).
  3. Growth: reaching RM3 million in YA2023 to YA2025 means 1 July 2026; reaching it in YA2026 or later means 1 January of the second year after that YA.
  4. Relaxation: the end of the interim relaxation period is shown for each start date stated by LHDN.

What counts as an e-invoice

  • An e-invoice is submitted to LHDN's MyInvois system (the free MyInvois Portal or software through the API) and validated with a unique ID and QR code. A PDF or paper invoice is not an e-invoice.
  • Buyers who do not ask for an e-invoice can be grouped into one consolidated e-invoice a month, submitted within 7 days after month-end.
  • A validated e-invoice can be cancelled within 72 hours; after that, use a credit or debit note.
  • Exempt? Keep making normal invoices and quotations with the free Invoice Generator.
