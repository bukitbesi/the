Sort Lines Alphabetically, by Number or Length

Paste one item per line and it is sorted A to Z as you type, in natural order so 2 comes before 10 ({{LN_NAT}}). Switch to Z to A, sort by the number in each line, by length, by last word or by a CSV column, remove duplicates, or shuffle the list at random.

How to sort lines

  1. Paste your list, one item per line.
  2. Choose the order: A to Z, Z to A, by number, by length, or press Shuffle.
  3. Choose what to sort by: the whole line, the last word, or a column of CSV or tab-separated data.
  4. Copy or download the sorted list. Tick Remove duplicates to clean it in the same step.

How the sorter compares lines

  • Collation: the browser's Unicode collation (Intl.Collator) for English or Malay, so accented letters sort next to their base letter (é with e) instead of after z.
  • Case: ignored by default; Case-sensitive puts capitals before lowercase for the same word (A, a, B, b).
  • Ties: lines that compare equal keep their original order (stable sort).
  • Numbers: the first number in the line or column is used; "1,050.50" is one thousand and fifty point five.

Note: Excel and Google Sheets sort numbers stored as text in plain character order (10 before 2). If your sorted list will go back into a spreadsheet, sort it there by a number column, or keep Natural order on here.
