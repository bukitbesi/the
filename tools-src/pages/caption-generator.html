Instagram cut hashtags from 30 to 5 (announced 18 December 2025); TikTok has allowed 5 since July 2025. TikTok's official caption limit is 2,200 characters, though many accounts get 4,000 in the app. X Premium allows 25,000. Characters before "more" are approximate.

How it works

  1. Your idea, platform, tone, language and occasion are sent to Cloudflare Workers AI (Mistral Small 3.1, Llama 3.3 70B if busy).
  2. The AI writes 5 numbered captions and a hashtag line; your browser splits them into cards.
  3. Hashtags are cleaned, de-duplicated and capped at the platform limit.
  4. Each caption is counted against the platform's character limit and flagged if too long.

Tips for captions that get engagement

  • Hook first: only about 125 characters show on Instagram before "more".
  • Be specific: add the place, price or offer in your description for sharper captions.
  • Fewer, better hashtags: 3 to 5 that describe the post beat a long generic list.
  • Need a picture too? Make one with the AI image generator or turn a photo into a cartoon with Photo to Cartoon.
