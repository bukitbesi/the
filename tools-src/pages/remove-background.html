Example: a product photo for Shopee

You photograph a handbag on a table. Choose Object mode (on a computer with Chrome), or Plain background if the table is one colour, then the White background, tick Crop to subject and download a JPG. The result is the handbag on clean white, cropped tight with a 2% margin, ready for an online shop.

How it works

  1. Segmentation: the AI model (MODNet or BiRefNet-lite) predicts, for every pixel, how much it belongs to the subject: 0 background, 255 subject, values in between for soft hair edges.
  2. Refine: edge strictness sets the threshold, grow/shrink dilates or erodes the mask, and soften edges blurs it slightly.
  3. Brush: Erase strokes lower the mask to 0 and Restore raises it to 255, with a soft edge.
  4. Composite: each pixel = subject × mask + new background × (1 − mask), or the alpha channel for a transparent PNG.

Tips for the best result

  • Contrast: a subject that differs in colour from the background gives the cleanest edge.
  • Hair: keep Soften edges at 1 to 2 px; higher values leave a halo.
  • Passport photos: use the Passport Photo Maker, which removes the background, places the head to the official size and lays out a 4R print sheet.
  • WhatsApp stickers: download a transparent PNG, then pack it with the WhatsApp Sticker Maker.

Sources

  • Xenova/modnet (Apache-2.0) and the MODNet paper
  • BiRefNet_lite-ONNX (MIT) and the BiRefNet paper
  • Transformers.js: runs the models in the browser with WebGPU or WebAssembly

Note: Only use photos you have the right to use. AI models can miss parts of a subject that matches the background colour; check the edges before using the result.
