How the PTPTN instalment is worked out

  1. Ujrah = principal × 1% × period (years). No compounding.
  2. Total repaid = principal + ujrah.
  3. Monthly instalment = total repaid ÷ (years × 12).
  4. Full settlement: the principal balance only, because ujrah is charged up to the current month.

Example: RM30,000 × 1% × 15 = RM4,500 ujrah; RM34,500 ÷ 180 months = RM191.67 a month.

PTPTN repayment rules

  • Start: 12 months after the loan ends, without waiting for a notice. Ujrah starts in the 13th month.
  • Discounts: no repayment discount under Budget 2026.
  • Restructuring: the period can be extended to age 60 with a minimum instalment of about 5–8% of gross income, subject to approval.
  • Salary deduction: employers can deduct instalments straight from salary; check your statement every year.
