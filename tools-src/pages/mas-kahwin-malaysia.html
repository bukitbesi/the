Mas Kahwin Malaysia 2026

The minimum mas kahwin is RM300 in Selangor and the Federal Territories, RM100 in Melaka and Pahang, and RM200 (anak dara) or RM100 (janda) in Negeri Sembilan; Terengganu and Perlis set no fixed rate. How this calculator works

  1. State rate: the minimum or fixed rate of the state where the nikah takes place, for anak dara or janda.
  2. Mas kahwin value: cash as is; gold = grams × fineness (0.999 or 0.916) × pure-gold price per gram; dinar = coins × 4.25 g × 0.917 × price per gram.
  3. Check: the value is compared with the state minimum.
  4. Wedding cost = mas kahwin + wang hantaran + gifts + (guests × catering per guest) + other + nikah fees; monthly saving = total ÷ months.

Nikah rules to know

  • Islamic pre-marriage course: compulsory, RM80 to RM120 by state (RM100 in Selangor).
  • HIV screening: compulsory for Muslim applicants at a government clinic; the result is valid for 6 months.
  • Deferred mas kahwin: allowed and recorded as "hutang" on the nikah form; set a payment date.
  • Registration: marrying without permission or not registering can be fined up to RM1,000, 6 months' jail or both. In Selangor, register within 6 months.
  • Wang hantaran: a custom, not a pillar of the nikah. If you need to borrow, work out the instalment first with the personal loan calculator.
