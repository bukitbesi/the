How the EPF dividend is calculated

EPF works out dividend on your daily balance and credits it once a year, after the rate is announced (usually in February or March).

  • Money that stays in your account the whole year earns the full rate: RM100,000 at {{E_LATEST}} earns RM6,150.
  • Each month's contribution earns only for the rest of the year. January's contribution earns about eleven months, December's almost nothing until the next year.
  • Dividend is credited to Akaun Persaraan, Sejahtera and Fleksibel on each account's own balance.

This calculator follows the same method month by month, then repeats it for every year until your chosen age, raising your salary each year.

Akaun Persaraan, Sejahtera and Fleksibel

Since 11 May 2024, every monthly contribution is split three ways:

  • Akaun Persaraan (75%) for retirement, withdrawable from age 55.
  • Akaun Sejahtera (15%) for approved withdrawals such as housing, education and health.
  • Akaun Fleksibel (10%) that you can withdraw at any time.

On RM1,000 of total monthly contributions, RM750 goes to Persaraan, RM150 to Sejahtera and RM100 to Fleksibel. Withdrawing from Fleksibel reduces both your balance and the dividend it would have earned.

How much EPF do you need to retire?

EPF's Retirement Income Adequacy framework sets three savings levels at age 60, in today's prices:
