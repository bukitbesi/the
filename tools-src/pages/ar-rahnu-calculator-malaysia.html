How Ar-Rahnu charges are worked out

  1. Gold value (marhun) = weight × purity × pure-gold price per gram.
  2. Financing = up to the provider's margin (80%) × gold value.
  3. Banks (tawarruq): monthly charge = financing × yearly rate ÷ 12.
  4. Safekeeping fee: monthly charge = gold value ÷ 100 × fee per RM100.

Things to know

  • Auction: if not redeemed or renewed, the gold is auctioned; any surplus is returned to you.
  • Tawarruq since 2020: Bank Negara's Shariah Advisory Council ruled that the qard, rahn, wadiah and ujrah structure does not meet the Rahn policy document for Islamic financial institutions.
  • Conventional pawnshops may charge up to 2% a month on the loan.
  • Accepted gold is usually 18K to 24K; check with the provider.
  • Pledged savings gold is still subject to zakat; work it out with the gold zakat calculator.
