How Malaysia's takwim is calculated

  1. Conjunction (ijtimak): the astronomical new moon, when the moon passes between the earth and the sun.
  2. MABIMS imkanur rukyah (from 1443 AH): at sunset after the conjunction, the crescent must be at least 3° high with at least 6.4° elongation. If so, the next day is the 1st; if not, the current month runs to 30 days.
  3. Moon sighting (rukyah): for Ramadan, Shawwal and Dhu al-Hijjah the crescent is observed at official sites and the date is announced by the Keeper of the Rulers' Seal.

This converter computes those criteria for Malaysia's western horizon and matches every official takwim date we checked from 1443 AH to 1449 AH, including Aidilfitri on 21 March 2026 and Awal Muharram 1448 AH on 17 June 2026. A Hijri year has 354 or 355 days, about 11 days shorter than a Gregorian year.
