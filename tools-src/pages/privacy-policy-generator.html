How to create and publish your privacy policy

  1. Fill in your website name, owner and contact email. The email is how people exercise their PDPA rights, so use one you check.
  2. Tick what your site really does: forms, comments, newsletter, accounts, orders, AdSense, Analytics, affiliate links.
  3. Choose Both for an English and Bahasa Melayu policy on one page, as section 7(3) of the PDPA asks.
  4. Read the preview and fix anything the checklist flags.
  5. Copy the HTML into a new Blogger or WordPress page called Privacy Policy and link it in your footer.

What Google requires

  • AdSense: say that third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits; that Google's advertising cookies let it and its partners serve ads; and how to opt out at Ads Settings or aboutads.info. If other ad networks serve ads on your site, name them and link to their opt-outs too.
  • Google Analytics: disclose that you use Google Analytics and how it collects and processes data.
