How to remove duplicate lines

  1. Paste your list: emails, phone numbers, keywords, URLs, product codes, one per line.
  2. Choose the match: exact, or ignore case and extra spaces.
  3. Choose what to keep: one copy of every line, only lines that appear once, or only the lines that repeat.
  4. Copy or download the cleaned list. The summary tells you how many lines went.

How lines are compared

  • Line endings: Windows (CR LF), old Mac (CR) and Linux (LF) line breaks are all read as one line break.
  • Spaces: spaces and tabs at the start and end of a line are ignored by default; untick Trim to compare them too. Ignore extra spaces also treats several spaces inside a line as one.
  • Case: exact by default. Ignore case uses the browser's Unicode lowercase, so accented and non-Latin letters match too.
  • Order: unlike the command-line uniq, duplicates do not have to be next to each other and the list is not sorted.

Note: "Remove line breaks" cannot tell a real line break (an address, a poem, a list) from a broken sentence. Check lists and addresses after cleaning, or clean only the paragraphs you need.
