How fidyah is worked out

  1. Delayed qada: days × Ramadans passed × rate. The fast must still be made up.
  2. Pregnant or nursing, for the child: days × (1 + Ramadans passed) × rate, and make up the fast.
  3. Unable to fast (old age or chronic illness): days × rate, with no qada.
