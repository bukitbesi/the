NCD moves at each renewal: every claim-free policy year takes you up one step. A new car starts at 0%, so the first 25% applies at the renewal after one full claim-free year.

How the premium after NCD is worked out

  1. NCD discount = gross premium × NCD percentage.
  2. Net premium = gross premium − NCD discount.
  3. SST = 8% × net premium.
  4. Total = net premium + SST + RM10 stamp duty.

Example: RM2,000 gross premium at 55% NCD → RM1,100 discount, RM900 premium, RM72 SST, RM982 in total.

Should you claim or pay yourself?

After a claim the NCD drops to 0% and takes five years to climb back to 55%. The extra premium over those years is what the claim really costs. If the repair costs less, paying yourself is cheaper.

NCD rules to know

  • The NCD is yours, not the car's. When you change cars, ask for an NCD withdrawal from the old policy to use on the new car in your name.
  • Own-damage, theft and disaster claims reset the NCD to 0% at the next renewal.
  • Windscreen claims under windscreen cover, and claims against the at-fault driver's insurer, usually leave the NCD intact.
  • Late renewal can cost you the NCD; renew before the policy expires.
  • Since motor premiums were liberalised, each insurer sets its own gross premium, but the same NCD scale still applies.
