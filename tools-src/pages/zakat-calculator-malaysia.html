How to calculate income zakat

  1. Add up yearly income: salary, bonus, allowances and other income.
  2. Choose a method: gross (simpler, 2.5% of total income) or net, after had kifayah and EPF.
  3. Compare with the nisab: the value of 85 g of gold set by your state zakat body (Selangor's nisab implies {{Z_GOLD}} a gram).
  4. Take 2.5%: if you reach the nisab, zakat = 2.5% × income liable to zakat.
  5. Claim the rebate: enter the zakat in the rebate section of LHDN e-Filing, with the zakat body's receipt.
