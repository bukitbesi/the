How Bursa trading costs are worked out

  1. Contract value = price × number of shares.
  2. Brokerage = the higher of value × your broker's rate and the broker's minimum.
  3. Clearing fee = 0.03% of value, up to RM1,000.
  4. Stamp duty = 0.1% of value, up to RM1,000.
  5. SST = 8% of brokerage and clearing for ETFs, REITs and warrants.
  6. Net profit = sale value − selling costs − (buy value + buying costs) + dividends.

Tax on shares in Malaysia

  • Dividends: single-tier dividends are tax-free up to RM100,000 a year; from YA2025 individuals pay 2% on the excess. Foreign dividends and EPF, ASNB and unit trust distributions are outside this rule.
  • Capital gains: gains on listed shares are not subject to capital gains tax for individuals.
  • Brokerage is negotiable between you and your broker; online brokers often charge a percentage with a low minimum.
