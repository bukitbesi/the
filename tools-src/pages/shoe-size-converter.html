How sizes are worked out

  1. Shoe last = foot length + 1.7 cm (adults) or + 1.2 cm (children).
  2. UK adult = 3 × last (inches) − 25; UK kids = 3 × last − 12.
  3. EU = 1.5 × last (cm); US men = UK + 1; US women = UK + 2.
  4. Mondopoint = foot length in mm.

Tips for choosing a size

  • Measure in the afternoon, when feet are largest, wearing the socks you will use.
  • Between two sizes? Take the larger, especially for sports and school shoes.
  • The cm on a shoe box is a size code, not a foot length.
  • Wide feet: pick a wide fit; width is measured across the widest part of the foot.
