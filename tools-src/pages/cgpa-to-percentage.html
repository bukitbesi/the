Worked example

A VTU student with a CGPA of 8.2 subtracts 0.75 to get 7.45, then multiplies by 10: 74.5%. The same CGPA is 77.9% with the CBSE formula, which is why using the right formula matters on job and admission forms.

Things to know

  • Your university's conversion certificate is the official figure. Request one from the examination section if a form asks for a percentage.
  • SGPA (one semester) converts with the same formula as CGPA at most universities.
  • Results below 0% are shown as 0%, and above 100% as 100%.
  • For a grading scale other than 10, choose "Any scale" or enter your university's formula as a custom formula.

Disclaimer: Formulas are as commonly published by each university or board and are provided for guidance. They are not an official conversion. {{BRAND}} is not affiliated with any university or board.
