How it works

  1. Your text, length and format are sent to Cloudflare Workers AI (Mistral Small 3.1, Llama 3.3 70B if busy).
  2. The model is told to use only what is in the text and keep names, numbers and dates.
  3. Your browser counts the words before and after: reading time = words ÷ 230 a minute.
  4. Nothing is saved: refresh the page and the text is gone.

Tips for a better summary

  • Remove clutter such as menus, adverts and comments before pasting a web page.
  • Very long documents: summarize each section, then summarize the summaries.
  • Check key facts against the original before quoting them.
  • Want to reword the summary in your own style? Use the paraphrasing tool.

Sources

  • Cloudflare Workers AI: Mistral Small 3.1 and free allocation
  • Purdue OWL: Quoting, paraphrasing and summarizing
  • Brysbaert (2019): reading rate meta-analysis (238 words a minute for silent reading of non-fiction)

Note: AI summaries can miss nuance or make mistakes. Check important details against the original, and cite the source when you use its ideas.
