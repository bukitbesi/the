How the retirement savings are worked out

  1. Expenses at retirement = today's monthly expenses × (1 + inflation)^years to retirement.
  2. Savings needed = the value at retirement of those expenses, rising with inflation, paid monthly until the age you plan for, with the rest earning the post-retirement return.
  3. Projected savings = savings now and monthly saving grown at the pre-retirement return.
  4. Extra saving = the shortfall spread over the months left.

EPF's retirement savings levels

  • Basic savings: RM390,000, raised from RM240,000 and phased in by 2028.
  • Adequate savings: RM650,000.
  • Enhanced savings: RM1.3 million.
  • Review: every three years from 2029, using the Belanjawanku study of living costs.
