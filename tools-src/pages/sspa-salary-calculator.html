How the SSPA salary is worked out

  1. Phase 1 = basic salary × 8% (RM240 minimum), or × 4% for Top Management.
  2. Salary on 31 December 2025 = salary after phase 1 + the 2025 increment.
  3. Phase 2 = salary on 31 December 2025 × 7% (or × 3%).
  4. New salary = salary on 31 December 2025 + phase 2. If it is still below the grade's starting salary, it is raised to the starting salary.

Other things to know

  • Gred 1 starting salary under SSPA is RM1,500 a month.
  • Annual increments are now set per grade and need a MyPERFORMANCE score of at least 75%; your actual salary also depends on them.
  • Pensioners who retired before 1 January 2026 receive the pension adjustment, not the phase 2 salary adjustment.
  • Contract officers are reported to qualify at the same rates, but only one source confirms it.
  • Work out take-home pay after EPF and PCB with the salary calculator, and your pension with the retirement age calculator.
