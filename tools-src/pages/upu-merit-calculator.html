UPU's official examples

  • STEM A (BM A+, English A, History A+, Mathematics A+, Physics A+, Chemistry A, Additional Mathematics A, Biology A-, Islamic Education A+): universal (18+16+18+18) ÷ 72 × 40 = 38.89; package (18+16) ÷ 36 × 30 = 28.33; best others (18+16) ÷ 36 × 10 = 9.44; academic (38.89 + 28.33 + 9.44) ÷ 80 × 90 = {{M_EXA}}; with 7 co-curriculum, merit {{M_EXM}}.
  • STEM C: universal 28.89, one package subject A- ÷ 18 × 30 = 23.33, best others 9.44 → academic {{M_EXCA}}, merit {{M_EXC}} with 9.5 co-curriculum.
  • Arts and Humanities (package Economics A-, Principles of Accounting A+): 32.22 + 26.67 + 5.56 → academic {{M_EXKA}}, merit {{M_EXK}} with 9.5 co-curriculum.

How it is calculated

  1. Universal (40): Bahasa Melayu + English + Mathematics + History ÷ 72 × 40.
  2. Package (30): the two best package subjects ÷ 36 × 30 (STEM C: one ÷ 18 × 30).
  3. Best others (10): the two best of the remaining subjects (including Islamic or Moral Education, Science and leftover package subjects) ÷ 36 × 10.
  4. Academic (90): (universal + package + best others) ÷ 80 × 90, each part rounded to 2 decimals as in UPU's examples.
  5. Merit: academic + co-curriculum (maximum 10).

The circular's STEM B example lists Bahasa Melayu as A+ but counts it as A (16), so it is not used as a test; this calculator follows the set grade values.

Other things to know

  • Merit is not the only test: you must also meet the programme's general and special requirements, minimum grades in set subjects, any interview or test, and the number of places.
  • Candidates are ranked by merit from highest to lowest, then offered places in the order of their choices.
  • Plan study costs: PTPTN Calculator and SSPN Calculator. University grades: CGPA Calculator.
