How fixed deposit interest is worked out

  1. Interest = deposit × yearly rate × months ÷ 12 (simple interest for one term).
  2. Maturity amount = deposit + interest.
  3. Monthly interest = deposit × rate ÷ 12.
  4. Renewals: each term's interest is added to the deposit, so it compounds.

What to know about FDs in Malaysia

  • PIDM: up to RM250,000 per depositor per member bank, interest included; Islamic and conventional separately.
  • Tax: interest from licensed banks is exempt for resident individuals.
  • OPR: {{D_OPR}}% (held on 3 September 2026); FD rates move with it.
  • Inflation: averaged {{D_INFL}}% in Q2 2026; compare your FD rate with inflation for the real return.
  • Early withdrawal: interest is cut or not paid; check the bank's terms.
