The prayer times explained

  • Imsak: a reminder to stop eating sahur, 10 minutes before Subuh (at Subuh in Perlis). Not a prayer.
  • Subuh (Fajr): from true dawn until syuruk.
  • Syuruk: sunrise, when Subuh time ends.
  • Dhuha: the start of the time for the voluntary dhuha prayer, after the sun has risen.
  • Zohor (Dhuhr): just after the sun passes its highest point; Friday prayers are held at this time.
  • Asar (Asr): in the afternoon, when shadows have lengthened.
  • Maghrib: just after sunset; the time to break the fast in Ramadan.
  • Isyak (Isha): when the twilight has gone, until Subuh.

How this prayer time calculator works

  1. Pick your zone or tap 'Use my location'. Your position is matched on your device to the nearest of 235 district centres and towns, and its JAKIM zone.
  2. The year's timetable for that zone loads once (a few kilobytes) from this site, then works offline.
  3. The countdown runs on Malaysia time (UTC+8) and moves to the next prayer at each azan; after Isyak it counts to tomorrow's Subuh.
  4. Your zone and clock format are remembered on this device, so the times are ready on your next visit.
