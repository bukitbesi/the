How to calculate CGPA

  1. Find each semester's SGPA and credits on your grade cards.
  2. Multiply each SGPA by that semester's credits.
  3. Add the results, then divide by the total number of credits.

The same method gives a semester GPA from course grades: multiply each course's grade point by its credits, add them up and divide by the total credits.

Worked example

Two semesters: SGPA 8.0 over 20 credits and SGPA 9.0 over 25 credits. (8.0 × 20) + (9.0 × 25) = 160 + 225 = 385. Divide by 45 credits: CGPA 8.56. A simple average would give 8.50, because it ignores that the second semester carried more credits.
