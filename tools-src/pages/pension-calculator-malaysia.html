JPA's official examples

  • Last salary RM5,440.60, 386 months: pension capped at 360 months, 60% × RM5,440.60 = {{P_EX1_PEN}} a month; gratuity 7.5% × RM5,440.60 × 386 = {{P_EX1_GRAT}} (gratuity has no month cap).
  • Last salary RM2,203.56, 255 months: pension 1/600 × 255 × RM2,203.56 = {{P_EX2_PEN}}; no minimum because it is under 300 months; gratuity {{P_EX2_GRAT}}.
  • The default example above: born 15 June 1966, appointed 1 July 1993, retiring 15 June 2026 (395 months), RM6,000 salary and RM800 fixed allowances: pension {{P_DEF_PEN}}, gratuity {{P_DEF_GRAT}}, GCR 180 ÷ 30 × RM6,800 = {{P_DEF_GCR}}, lump sum {{P_DEF_LUMP}}.

How it is calculated

  1. Service = whole months from the appointment date to the day before retirement; at least 120 months (10 years).
  2. Pension = 1/600 × months (up to 360) × last salary; RM1,000 if lower and service is 300 months or more.
  3. Gratuity = 7.5% × all months × last salary.
  4. GCR = leave days (up to 180) ÷ 30 × (last salary + fixed allowances).
  5. When paid: gratuity and GCR on the retirement date; the pension from the retirement date or age 55 (appointed from 12 April 1991), whichever is later.

SSPA pension adjustment for pensioners (360 months, retired before December 2024)

{{P_SSPA}}
Last salaryPhase 1 (1/12/2024)Phase 2 (1/1/2026)Pension before → after
  • Phase 1: last salary over RM3,000 +8%, under RM3,000 +RM240, JUSA +4%. At exactly RM3,000 both methods give RM240, so the answer is the same.
  • Phase 2: +7%, JUSA +3%. Those who retired between 2 December 2024 and 1 January 2026 get Phase 2 only.
  • Retired before 2013: JPA uses the "2012 equivalent last salary", not your actual last pay. Enter the figure from MyPesara.
  • PKKP (for those who retired by 31 December 2022) is added after the formula.

Other rules to know

  • Retirement age: compulsory at 60 for appointments from 1 January 2012; earlier appointees may have chosen 55, 56, 58 or 60. For the date and EPF ages, use the Retirement Age Calculator.
  • Fixed allowances for GCR: include ITKA, ITP, ITK and ITJU; the cost-of-living aid (BSH) is normally not counted. Confirm with your HR unit.
  • Derivative pension: if a pensioner dies, dependants qualify under the same formula; each widow or widower gets 2 shares and each child 1 share.
  • Special payment: Budget 2027 announced RM750 for pensioners in 2027, paid in two instalments.
  • Your current SSPA pay: SSPA Salary Calculator.
