How the generator works

  1. Payload: your details are written in the format phone cameras recognise, for example WIFI:, mailto: or a wa.me link.
  2. Encoding: the text is encoded as UTF-8 bytes (so Malay, Chinese and Tamil work) and the smallest QR version that fits is chosen.
  3. Error correction: Reed–Solomon codes at the level you pick let scanners read a scratched or partly covered code.
  4. Drawing: the modules are drawn as SVG with your colours and style; the three corner squares stay solid so phones lock on quickly.

Tips for QR codes that scan first time

  • Dark on light: light codes on dark backgrounds (inverted) fail on some scanners; the generator warns you.
  • Keep the border: leave the white margin of 4 modules; do not crop it when designing.
  • Short is better: a short link gives a less dense code that scans from further away.
  • Test before printing: scan with an Android phone and an iPhone at the real size.
