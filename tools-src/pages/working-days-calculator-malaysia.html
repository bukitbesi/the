How working days are counted

  1. Calendar days from the start date to the end date, both included.
  2. Minus weekends: Friday and Saturday in Kedah, Kelantan and Terengganu; Saturday and Sunday elsewhere.
  3. Minus public holidays on workdays, federal and state, with replacement days.
  4. Minus annual leave you plan to take.
  5. Deadline: step forward from the start date, counting only working days.

Public holiday rules

  • Weekends: Kedah, Kelantan and Terengganu rest on Friday and Saturday; Johor moved to Saturday and Sunday on 1 January 2025.
  • Extra Hari Raya holiday 2026: Friday 20 March 2026 nationwide, plus Thursday 19 March in Kelantan.
  • Islamic dates follow the official moon sighting and can shift by a day.
  • Employees: the Employment Act 1955 gives 11 paid public holidays a year, five of them fixed; employers pick the rest from gazetted holidays.
