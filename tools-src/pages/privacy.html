Privacy Policy

Effective {{DATE_TEXT}}

This policy explains how {{BRAND}} handles information on {{ORIGIN}} ("this site"). Tools listed here that open on {{MAIN_SITE}} are covered by that site's privacy policy.

Information you enter into calculators

Calculators on this site, including the salary calculator, run entirely in your browser. The salary, reliefs and other figures you enter are not sent to us or stored on a server. When you copy a result link, your inputs are kept in the part of the address after the # sign, which browsers do not send to websites.

What we do not collect

This site has no user accounts, sign-up forms or comment system, and it does not use analytics or tracking scripts of its own.

Information stored on your device

  • Theme preference: if you switch between light and dark mode, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage under the name tbb-theme.
  • Offline copy: a service worker saves this site's pages, scripts and icons in your browser's cache so they open without an internet connection.

Neither is shared with us. You can remove both at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical information such as IP addresses in server logs to operate and secure the service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Advertising and cookies

This site may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on those visits.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. Ad scripts load only after you first interact with a page.

Links to other websites

This site links to tools on {{MAIN_SITE}} and to official sources such as KWSP, PERKESO and LHDN. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your rights under Malaysian law

This site does not collect personal data through its pages. If you contact us, we use your details only to reply, in line with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010. You may ask us to access, correct or delete that correspondence.
