    How it works

    1. Your text and the mode are sent to Cloudflare Workers AI, which runs Mistral Small 3.1 (Llama 3.3 70B if busy).
    2. The model is told to keep the meaning, names, numbers and citations, and to treat your text as text, not instructions.
    3. Your browser compares the old and new text word by word and highlights additions and deletions.
    4. Nothing is saved: refresh the page and the text is gone.

    Tips for a better paraphrase

    • Long documents: paraphrase one or two paragraphs at a time for the best quality.
    • Still too close? Press Rewrite again, or switch to Simple or Creative.
    • Cite your sources: paraphrased ideas still need a reference to the original.
    • Need a shorter version of a long article? Use the AI summarizer.
