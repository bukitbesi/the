How refinancing is worked out

  1. Instalment = loan × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)−n), where r = rate ÷ 12 and n = months.
  2. Interest saved = total interest on the old loan − interest on the new loan for the same balance.
  3. Costs = 0.5% stamp duty + SRO 2023 legal fees + 8% SST + penalty + valuation + other.
  4. Break-even = costs ÷ monthly instalment saving.

When refinancing pays off

  • The new rate is at least 0.5% below your current rate.
  • You are past the lock-in period, or the penalty is small next to the saving.
  • You will keep the house longer than the break-even period.
  • A longer tenure lowers the instalment but adds interest; compare total interest, not just the instalment.
