Pos Laju Tracking

Enter your 13-character Pos Laju tracking number, two or three letters, nine digits and MY at the end (such as ER123456789MY), to open Pos Malaysia's official tracking page with the number copied. Below: what each Pos Laju status means and what to do when your parcel waits at the post office.

Pos Laju tracking number format

Pos Malaysia uses the Universal Postal Union's S10 standard, the same format postal services use worldwide:

  • 2 letters for the service, then 8 digits for the item, then 1 check digit, then the country code MY.
  • Because the last digit is a check digit, one mistyped digit usually returns "no result" instead of someone else's parcel. Copy and paste the number rather than typing it.
  • Some Pos Laju labels use three letters at the start, such as EHE108177925MY; they track the same way.
What the first letter usually means
Starts withService
EExpress mail and courier (Pos Laju, EMS)
RRegistered mail
CParcel post
LTracked small packets, often from overseas sellers (for example LP…CN)

A number ending in another country code, such as CN or SG, was posted abroad. Pos Malaysia delivers it once it reaches Malaysia, so its Malaysian leg shows on Pos Malaysia's tracking page too.

Pos Laju tracking statuses explained

StatusWhat it means
Item posted over the counterThe sender handed the parcel in at a counter and paid postage. The journey starts here.
Item dispatched outThe parcel left a processing centre. It appears each time the parcel moves on, so you may see it several times.
Item arrived / processed at a centreThe parcel reached the next sorting centre on its route.
Item out for deliveryA courier has the parcel for delivery, usually that same day.
Ready for collection at the counterDelivery was not completed, or your area is served by counter collection. The parcel waits at the named post office.
Item deliveredPos Laju recorded the delivery.

Exact wording differs slightly between Pos Malaysia's website and app, but the order of events is the same.

Collecting a Pos Laju parcel from the post office

  1. Check the tracking page for the post office holding your parcel.
  2. Bring the tracking number, any collection notice left at your door and your MyKad or passport.
  3. If someone collects for you, ask the outlet what it needs; usually an authorisation letter and copies of both identity cards.
  4. Collect before the date on the notice. Uncollected parcels are returned to the sender.
