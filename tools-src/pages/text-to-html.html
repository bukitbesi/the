How to convert text to HTML

  1. Paste your text. Leave a blank line between paragraphs.
  2. Start list lines with - or 1. to get a bulleted or numbered list.
  3. Choose the options: line breaks, links, new tab, nofollow.
  4. Check the preview, then copy the HTML code or download a complete page.
  5. Paste into the HTML view of Blogger, WordPress or your email tool.
