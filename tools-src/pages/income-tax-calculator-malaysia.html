How Malaysian income tax is calculated

  1. Add up your income for the year: employment income (your EA form) plus any other income.
  2. Deduct approved donations (up to 10% of income) and your reliefs, each up to its limit. The result is your chargeable income.
  3. Apply the tax rates band by band to your chargeable income.
  4. Subtract rebates: RM400 if chargeable income is RM35,000 or less (RM800 with a non-working spouse), then zakat paid.
  5. Compare with PCB deducted by your employer to see whether you get a refund or owe a balance.
