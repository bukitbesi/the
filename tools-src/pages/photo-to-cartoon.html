How the cartoon is made

  1. Smooth the photo with a Kuwahara filter, which flattens skin and walls but keeps edges sharp.
  2. Pick the colours with k-means clustering: similar colours are grouped and each group becomes one flat paint colour.
  3. Draw outlines where brightness changes sharply (Sobel edge detection), thickened to the width you choose.
  4. Combine the flat colours and dark lines. The pencil sketch instead divides the photo by a blurred negative of itself (colour dodge).

Tips for the best cartoon

  • Faces: use a well-lit photo, then Soft anime or Classic cartoon with smoothness 4 to 6.
  • Busy backgrounds create many lines; lower line strength or choose Painting.
  • Stickers: cartoonize, then cut out and pack them with the WhatsApp sticker maker.
  • Want an AI-made picture instead? Create one with the AI Image Generator, or describe a photo with Image to Prompt.

Sources

  • Kuwahara filter, k-means clustering and Sobel operator
  • W3C: Color dodge blending and MDN: Canvas pixel data

Note: Only cartoonize photos you have the right to use, and ask permission before sharing a cartoon of another person.
