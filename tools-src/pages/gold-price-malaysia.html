Why are there three gold prices?

  • Spot price: the world market price of pure gold in USD, converted to ringgit. It moves every minute and underlies every other price.
  • Kijang Emas: Bank Negara's official price for its 999.9 gold coins, announced every working day. The gap between its selling and buying prices is your cost if you buy and later sell back.
  • Shop price: the gold value plus a premium and a making charge. Shops buy back below what they sell for.

How to use today's gold price

  1. Pick the purity: 999 for bars and coins, 916 for most jewellery.
  2. Multiply by the weight: weight in grams × the price per gram for that purity = gold value.
  3. Add the making charge: shops charge per gram or by design.
  4. Compare: enter the shop's price to see the premium you are paying.
