How the EIS allowance is worked out

  1. Assumed wage = your insured monthly wage, up to RM6,000.
  2. Job Search Allowance = 80%, 50%, 40%, 40%, 30% and 30% of it in months 1 to 6.
  3. Period: 3 to 6 months, set by PERKESO from your contributions.
  4. Early Re-employment Allowance = 50% of the unpaid allowance (25% for job loss before 1 October 2026).

EIS benefits and conditions (2026)

  • Eligibility: at least 12 contributions in the 24 months before job loss for a first claim; claim within 60 days at LINDUNG KERJAYA and register on MYFutureJobs.
  • Covered: retrenchment, VSS or MSS, business closure, constructive dismissal and resignation because of harassment, threats or dangerous orders.
  • Not covered: resignation, dismissal for misconduct and retirement.
  • From 1 October 2026: training fees up to RM7,000 with RM30 a day; Mobility Assistance Allowance of RM1,000 for a new job over 100 km from home within 12 months of the last allowance payment.
  • Also claim your employer's termination benefits; see the termination benefits calculator.
