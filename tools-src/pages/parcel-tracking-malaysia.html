How to track a parcel in Malaysia

  1. Find the tracking number. It is on your order page (Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop), in the seller's message or SMS, or printed above the barcode on the consignment note.
  2. Paste it above and press Track. Spaces and dashes are removed automatically.
  3. Open the courier's page. The number is copied when you tap the link, so you can paste it if the page opens empty.
  4. Not recognised? Pick the courier from the list, or use 17TRACK, which checks over a thousand couriers at once.

Parcel not moving? What to do

  • No update for 24 hours after the label was made: normal; the first scan happens at pick-up.
  • Stuck at one hub for 3 or more working days: contact the courier through its official website or app with the tracking number.
  • Marketplace orders: raise it in the Shopee, Lazada or TikTok Shop order page before the order's protection period ends; the platform can chase the courier for you.
  • Marked delivered but not received: report it to the seller and courier the same day; many couriers keep a delivery photo or signature.
