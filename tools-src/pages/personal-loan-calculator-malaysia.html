Personal loan rules in Malaysia

  • Flat rate ends {{Q_BAN}}: Bank Negara's revised fair treatment policy bans flat-rate pricing and the Rule of 78 for new personal financing; loans move to reducing balance with the effective rate disclosed.
  • Stamp duty: 0.5% of the loan amount on the agreement.
  • Licensed moneylenders: capped at 12% a year secured and 18% a year unsecured. Check the KPKT licence first.
  • Above RM100,000: from January 2027, a short financial education module comes before approval.
  • Home loan top-ups count as personal loans: from 1 January 2027, extra financing above the original home loan amount, or secured on a fully paid property, for personal use is treated as personal financing, with a maximum tenure of 10 years and the same DSR checks.

The formulas: flat-rate interest = loan × rate × years and instalment = (loan + interest) ÷ months; a reducing-balance instalment uses the standard annuity formula at the EIR.
