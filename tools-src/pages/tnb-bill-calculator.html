How the TNB bill is worked out (RP4 tariff)

  1. Base charges: kWh × (27.03 sen energy + 4.55 sen capacity + 12.85 sen network) = 44.43 sen per kWh.
  2. EEI rebate: your usage band's rate × all kWh, for use up to 1,000 kWh.
  3. AFA: kWh × the month's AFA rate, only above the protection level.
  4. Retail charge: RM10 a month, only above the protection level.
  5. KWTBB: 1.6% for use above 300 kWh (Renewable Energy Fund).
  6. 8% SST: on the part of the bill for kWh above the protection level.

Example for 300 kWh: 300 × 44.43 sen = RM133.29, less EEI of 300 × 22.5 sen = RM67.50, a bill of RM65.79 (21.9 sen/kWh on average).

800 kWh protection to December 2026

For usage from September to 31 December 2026, the government raised the domestic protection level from 600 to 800 kWh a month. Households up to 800 kWh pay no AFA, retail charge or SST. Example for 800 kWh with a 3 sen AFA: {{T_800P}} with the protection, against {{T_800N}} at the standard level.

How to cut your electricity bill

  • Set air conditioners to 24–25°C and clean the filters monthly.
  • Watch the kWh on your bill: going past 600 or 800 kWh adds AFA, the retail charge and SST, and the EEI rebate shrinks as use rises.
  • Switch to LED lights and Energy Commission 5-star rated appliances.
