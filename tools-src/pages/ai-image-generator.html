How it works

  1. Optional: Improve prompt asks a language model to translate and expand your idea into a 40 to 70 word English prompt.
  2. The chosen style's words are added to the end of your prompt.
  3. Cloudflare Workers AI runs FLUX.1 [schnell] for 4 steps (fast) or 8 steps (detailed) and returns the picture.
  4. Your browser shows it and keeps the last 6 in this tab; nothing is saved on the server.

Tips for better AI pictures

  • Describe, don't command: "a wooden kampung house at misty dawn, wide shot" works better than "make a house".
  • Add lighting and camera: golden hour, neon night, close-up, aerial view.
  • Text in pictures is often misspelled; add words later in an editor.
  • Build detailed prompts with the AI Prompt Generator, or get one from a photo with Image to Prompt.
