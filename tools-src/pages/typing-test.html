Example: working out WPM

In a 1-minute test you type 250 characters, including spaces. That is 250 ÷ 5 = {{Y_EX_GROSS}} gross WPM. You left 3 mistakes uncorrected, so net WPM is 50 − 3 = {{Y_EX_NET}} WPM. You pressed 260 keys, 13 of them wrong (some later fixed with Backspace), so accuracy is 247 ÷ 260 = {{Y_EX_ACC}}%.

How the test works

  1. Text: common words, sentences or numbers are picked at random; every test is different, and Retry repeats the same text.
  2. Clock: it starts on your first key and stops at 15 s, 30 s, 1 min or 2 min, or when you finish the text.
  3. Gross WPM: characters typed ÷ 5 ÷ minutes. Net WPM: gross minus uncorrected errors per minute.
  4. Accuracy: correct keystrokes ÷ all keystrokes, so a mistake you fixed still counts once.

Typing in Malaysian government jobs

  • Applying (SPA): Pembantu Tadbir (Perkeranian/Operasi) N1 and Pembantu Setiausaha Pejabat N1 ask for SPM with a credit in Bahasa Melayu; no typing speed is set.
  • Service examination (JPA), Pembantu Setiausaha Pejabat N1: take notes at 30 words a minute, then type the transcript at 20 words a minute with at most 4% errors.
  • Setiausaha Pejabat N5: notes at 50 words a minute, typing at 20 words a minute, at most 4% errors, plus the ISAC computer test. Pembantu Tadbir N1 takes ISAC only.

How to type faster

  • Home row: left fingers on A S D F, right fingers on J K L ;. Feel the bumps on F and J, and return after every key.
  • Eyes on the screen: looking down at the keys is the biggest brake on speed.
  • Accuracy first: fixing mistakes costs more time than typing slightly slower.
  • Short daily practice: a few 1-minute tests a day, then work on the keys this test says you miss most.
