How the SSPN calculator works

  1. Deposits are added at the start of each month; a lump sum at the start of year 1.
  2. Dividend = yearly rate × average month-end balance, credited at the end of each year.
  3. Tax relief = this year's deposits, up to RM8,000.
  4. Tax saved = tax on chargeable income − tax after the relief (YA2026 resident rates).
