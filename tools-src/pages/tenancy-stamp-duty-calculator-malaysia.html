Tenancy Agreement Stamp Duty Calculator

Stamp duty on a Malaysian tenancy agreement is RM1, RM3, RM5 or RM7 for every RM250 of yearly rent for a term up to 1, 3, 5 or more than 5 years, with no RM2,400 exemption and a RM10 minimum. A 2-year tenancy at RM1,500 a month pays {{T_DUTY}}, plus RM10 for a copy. Enter your rent and term below.

How tenancy stamp duty is worked out

  1. Yearly rent = monthly rent × 12 (the average yearly rent if it changes).
  2. Units = yearly rent ÷ RM250, rounded up.
  3. Duty = units × the rate for the term, at least RM10.
  4. Copies: RM10 each.

Stamp duty rates for tenancy agreements

{{T_BANDS}}
Tenancy termDuty per RM250 of yearly rent
  • Old rule: the first RM2,400 of yearly rent was exempt and the rates were RM1, RM2 and RM4 per RM250. Most sources date the change to 1 January 2025; a few say 1 January 2026. Agreements signed in 2026 use the new rates either way.
  • Self-assessment: from 1 January 2026 tenancy agreements are stamped through e-Duti Setem on MyTax, within 30 days of signing.
  • Rental income tax: net rent is taxed at normal rates; the 50% exemption applied only to YA2018.
