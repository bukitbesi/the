How to convert a list to a paragraph

  1. Paste your list, one item per line. Bullets (-, •, *), numbers (1. 2) (3)), letters (a.) and check boxes are removed automatically.
  2. Choose how to join the items: commas with a final "and" or "or", plain commas, semicolons, sentences or your own separator.
  3. Add quotes if the line is for SQL or a spreadsheet.
  4. Copy the result, or press Use result as text to keep working on it.

Rules the converter follows

  • Oxford comma: on by default (Chicago Manual of Style, APA); untick it for AP and most British style. Two items never get a comma: "tea and coffee".
  • CSV quoting: double quotes inside an item are doubled (say "hi" becomes "say ""hi"""), as in RFC 4180.
  • Sentence splitting: a full stop, question mark or exclamation mark followed by a space ends a sentence, except after common abbreviations (Dr., Prof., Sdn., Bhd., e.g., a.m.) or before a lowercase word.
  • Final conjunction: "and", "or", "dan" and "atau" after the last comma separate the last two items; "and" inside an item such as "salt and pepper" is kept.

Note: the converter does not change capitalisation. To make the joined paragraph sentence case, run it through the case converter.
