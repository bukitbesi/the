A 6 kWp system on a 600 kWh-a-month home. Exports above the month's imports are forfeited, so running loads in the daytime (air conditioning, washing machine, water heater) gives the best return.

How the solar bill is worked out

  1. Generation = kWp × yearly yield ÷ 12; self-use = generation × share used on site.
  2. Imports = use − self-use; exports = generation − self-use.
  3. Credit = the lowest of exports, imports and kWac × 5 hours × 30 days, at 27.03 sen/kWh.
  4. Bill = energy, capacity and network charges on imports − credit − EEI, plus AFA, retail charge, KWTBB and SST where they apply; never below zero.

Solar ATAP rules, 2026

  • Apply through a registered PV service provider on SEDA's eATAP portal; there is no quota or closing date.
  • No carry-over: credit not used in the billing month is forfeited, and a negative bill is set to zero.
  • 10-year contract: after that the system is for self-consumption only.
  • Strata homes (condominiums, apartments) are reported as not eligible.
  • Work out your TNB bill without solar with the TNB bill calculator.
