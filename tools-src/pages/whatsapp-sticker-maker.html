    How to make WhatsApp stickers

    1. Add photos (up to {{ST_MAX}}). Plain backgrounds are removed automatically.
    2. Touch up with the eraser and restore brushes; adjust the tolerance and press Remove background to try again.
    3. Frame it: drag to move, zoom and rotate, then set the outline and shadow. Add a caption if you want a meme sticker.
    4. Tag it with up to 3 emoji so WhatsApp's sticker search can find it.
    5. Download one sticker, or the whole pack once it has at least {{ST_MIN}} stickers.

    Getting the stickers into WhatsApp

    • WhatsApp Web or Desktop: open a chat, choose the attach menu's Sticker option and pick the downloaded image; WhatsApp sends it as a sticker you can then save as a favourite.
    • On a phone: recent versions of WhatsApp have a Create option in the sticker tab for single stickers. For a whole pack in your sticker tray, use a sticker pack app that imports WebP files; the pack's 512 px WebP files and tray icon are in the format those apps expect.
    • Developers: the ZIP's contents.json follows WhatsApp's official sample sticker app, so the folder can go straight into its assets to publish your own sticker app.

    Use pictures you have the right to use. WhatsApp requires stickers to be legal, authorised and acceptable. Do not turn other people's photos, artwork or brands into stickers without permission. This tool is free, provided as is, and not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.
