How the water bill is worked out

  1. Band 1: the first 20 m³ at the band 1 rate.
  2. Band 2: water from 20 to 35 m³ at the band 2 rate.
  3. Band 3: water above 35 m³ at the band 3 rate.
  4. Minimum charge: if the total is lower, you pay the minimum.

What to know about water tariffs

  • SPAN review: the national water regulator reviews tariffs under its tariff-setting mechanism; the latest round took effect from August 2025 (Penang deferred to July 2026).
  • Free water: some states give eligible households free water, such as Selangor's Skim Air Darul Ehsan (20 m³ a month) and Terengganu for B40 households; it is not deducted here.
  • Not included: Perak, Negeri Sembilan, Pahang, Perlis, Labuan, Sabah and Sarawak, because their current full tables could not be confirmed.
  • Save water: staying within 20 m³ a month keeps you in the cheapest band.
