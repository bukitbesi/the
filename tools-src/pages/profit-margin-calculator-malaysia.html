How profit margin is worked out

  1. Fees = selling price × fee % + fixed fee per order.
  2. Profit per unit = selling price − cost − fees − shipping you pay − ad cost per unit.
  3. Margin = profit ÷ selling price; markup = profit ÷ cost.
  4. Break-even price = (cost + fixed costs) ÷ (1 − fee %).
  5. Price for a target margin = (cost + fixed costs) ÷ (1 − fee % − target margin).

Pricing tips for Malaysian online sellers

  • Use your real fee rate: marketplace commission, payment and service fees differ by category, campaign and programme; take them from your seller centre.
  • Count free shipping and vouchers: any shipping subsidy or seller voucher is a cost per order.
  • Price on margin, not markup: a 30% markup is only a 23.1% margin, before fees.
  • SST is not profit: registered businesses collect it for RMCD.
