How assessment tax is worked out

  1. Annual value = estimated gross monthly rent × 12, set by the council's valuation department.
  2. Yearly tax = annual value × the council's rate (at most 35%).
  3. Each half = yearly tax ÷ 2, due 28 February and 31 August.
  4. Selangor: increases from 2025 are capped at 25% of the old tax.

Key points

  • Objections: according to MBPP, the rate cannot be appealed; objections are against the annual value when a new valuation list is reviewed.
  • Late payment: arrears after 28 February or 31 August; notice and warrant charges differ by council.
  • Selangor exemptions: low-cost and SelangorKu homes are exempt; some village homes can apply.
  • Johor and Melaka use improved value (market value) at a small rate, so this calculator does not fit councils there.
  • Not listed because sources are not yet sufficient: MBSA, MBSJ, MBKj, Putrajaya and other councils. Use the rate on your bill.
  • Other home costs: home loan, water bill and tenancy stamp duty.
