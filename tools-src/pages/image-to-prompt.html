    How the picture is read

    1. In your browser: the picture is shrunk to 560 pixels and its main colours, brightness and shape (aspect ratio) are measured. This always works, even offline.
    2. Online AI: Llama 3.2 11B Vision, running on Cloudflare Workers AI, describes the subject, action, setting, style, lighting, framing, mood and colours.
    3. In-browser AI (backup): if the online AI is busy, you can download a small captioning model once (about 250 MB). It writes a short caption on your device.
    4. Prompts: the description becomes 3 prompts in your app's format. Edit any field and they update instantly.

    Example: for a photo of a red scooter outside old shophouses, the Midjourney prompt is {{P_MJ}}.

    Tips for a closer match

    • Name the medium. Change "photo" to "35mm film photo" or "watercolor painting" if that is what you see.
    • Keep the shape. The aspect ratio is set from your picture; Midjourney gets it as --ar.
    • Add what makes it special. Brand-free details such as fabric, weather or time of day matter more than adjectives like "beautiful".
    • Generate a few times. Even the same prompt gives a different picture each run.

    How to use Image to Prompt

    1. Choose, drop or paste a picture (JPG, PNG or WebP).
    2. Wait a few seconds while the AI describes it, or use the in-browser AI if asked.
    3. Pick Image or Video and your app.
    4. Edit the description and copy one of the 3 prompts, or save it.
    5. Press Edit in Prompt Generator to add style, lighting and camera chips.
