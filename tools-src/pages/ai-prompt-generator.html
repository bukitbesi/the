    How each app reads a prompt

    • Midjourney: short comma phrases, then parameters: --ar for the shape, --no for things to leave out, --stylize (0–1000) for how artistic.
    • Stable Diffusion: comma keywords, most important first, with a separate negative prompt. Only about the first 75 tokens are read.
    • ChatGPT/DALL·E, Flux, Gemini: full sentences, subject first. There is no negative prompt; say what you want instead.
    • Veo and Sora: one shot with the camera move, plus sound and any spoken line in quotes.
    • Runway: the camera move first, then the scene; keep every instruction positive.
    • Kling: a descriptive scene in the main box and short, specific negatives in its negative prompt box.

    How to use the AI Prompt Generator

    1. Pick Image or Video and the app you will paste into.
    2. Type the subject, and optionally the action and setting. Or press Surprise me for a random idea.
    3. Tap chips for style, lighting, framing, mood and colour (camera movement and speed for video).
    4. Compare the 3 versions, copy the one you like, and save favourites for later.
    5. Share the link: it rebuilds exactly the same prompt for anyone who opens it.
