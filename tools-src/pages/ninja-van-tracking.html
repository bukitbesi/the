Ninja Van tracking ID format

  • Malaysian Ninja Van tracking IDs usually start with NV, NVMY or MYNJV.
  • Length varies, typically 11 to 18 letters and digits, because part of the ID comes from the shipper.
  • Some marketplace orders show the seller's or platform's own reference instead; the Ninja Van ID is under the order's shipping information.

Ninja Van tracking statuses explained

StatusWhat it means
Pending pickupThe shipper booked the delivery; Ninja Van has not collected the parcel yet.
In transit / At sorting hubThe parcel is moving between Ninja Van facilities.
On vehicle for deliveryA driver has the parcel and is delivering it today.
DeliveredNinja Van recorded the delivery.
Re-attemptThe first delivery failed; Ninja Van will try again.
To collectThe parcel was left at a Ninja Point for you to collect.
Returned to senderDelivery could not be completed and the parcel went back to the shipper.

Ninja Points: pick-up and drop-off

Ninja Points are partner shops where you can drop off parcels, collect missed deliveries and send returns. Ninja Van says there are over 5,500 across Malaysia.

  1. When tracking shows To collect, the page names the Ninja Point holding your parcel.
  2. Bring the tracking ID and your identity card, and go during the shop's opening hours.
  3. Collect promptly; parcels left too long are returned to the sender.
