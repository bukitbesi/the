How to find and replace or clean text

  1. Paste your text into the box.
  2. Type what to find and what to replace it with. Matches are highlighted and counted as you type. Tick Aa for match case, Word for whole words or .* for regex.
  3. Add rules for more replacements; they run in order from top to bottom.
  4. Press Replace all, or tick clean-ups and press Clean text.
  5. Copy the result. Undo steps back through every change.
