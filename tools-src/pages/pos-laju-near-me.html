Where you can send a Pos Laju parcel

  • Post offices (Pejabat Pos): counters accept Pos Laju parcels as well as ordinary and registered mail.
  • Pos Laju centres and outlets: parcel-focused counters, often in towns and shopping areas.
  • Collection: a parcel that could not be delivered waits at the post office named on its tracking page.

Post office opening hours

Post office counters usually keep office hours on working days, with shorter hours or no service on rest days and public holidays. Rest days also differ by state: some states, such as Kedah, Kelantan and Terengganu, rest on Friday and Saturday instead of Saturday and Sunday. Check the outlet's hours on Google Maps before you go.

Sending a parcel with Pos Laju

  1. Pack and seal the parcel; the counter weighs it to set the price.
  2. Have the recipient's name, phone number and full address with postcode ready.
  3. Pay and keep the receipt. Its 13-character number, ending in MY, is the tracking number.

Collecting a parcel? Bring the tracking number, any collection notice and your MyKad. See the collection steps.
