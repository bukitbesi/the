How the cost is worked out

  1. EV energy = distance × kWh/100 km ÷ 100.
  2. Home charging = TNB bill with the EV − bill without it (RP4 tariff, including the lost energy efficiency incentive).
  3. Public charging = remaining kWh × price per kWh.
  4. Petrol = litres × RM1.99 up to 300 litres (BUDI95), the unsubsidised price above that.

Key points

  • 100% home charging: {{E_EX_ALLHOME}} a month; 100% public: {{E_EX_ALLPUB}}, for the 1,500 km example.
  • TNB Time-of-Use (smart meter): off-peak energy 24.43 sen against 27.03 sen; peak is 2pm to 10pm on weekdays.
  • EV road tax applies from 1 January 2026; work it out with the road tax calculator.
  • Tax relief of up to RM2,500 for EV charging equipment and public charging subscriptions (LHDN).
  • See also the TNB bill calculator and the fuel cost calculator.
