J&T waybill number format

  • J&T calls the tracking number a waybill number. Each waybill covers one parcel.
  • Malaysian J&T waybills are usually 12 digits with no letters.
  • FedEx also uses 12-digit numbers, so the tracker shows both. For a local online order, J&T is almost always the right one.
  • On Shopee, Lazada or TikTok Shop, the waybill appears under the order's shipping information once the seller ships.

J&T tracking statuses explained

StatusWhat it means
Picked upA rider collected the parcel, or the sender dropped it at a J&T drop point.
ArrivalThe parcel reached a transit centre or gateway.
DepartureThe parcel left that centre for the next one, or for your area's drop point.
On deliveryA rider has the parcel for delivery to you.
DeliveredJ&T recorded the delivery.
On holdProgress is paused, for example because the drop point is closed, bad weather, or an address problem.
ReturnThe parcel is going back to the sender.

Why J&T tracking has gaps

J&T scans a parcel at key points, such as pick-up, each transit centre and delivery, not continuously. A day without an update on a long route, or between Peninsular and East Malaysia, is normal.

When to chase a J&T parcel

  1. No movement for more than 48 hours (longer after public holidays and sales such as 11.11).
  2. Check the waybill and your address on the order page first.
  3. Ask the seller: sellers who ship often have a direct contact at J&T.
  4. Then contact J&T through its official website or app with the waybill number.

About J&T Express Malaysia

According to the company, J&T Express entered Malaysia in January 2018 and launched officially in August 2018. It runs a nationwide network of drop points, where you can send a parcel over the counter, and transit gateways.
