Example: copying a receipt for a claim

Photograph a printed receipt flat on the table, straight on and in good light. Drop the photo here with English and Malay ticked. The shop name, items and total appear in the text box; check the numbers against the receipt (OCR can confuse 0 and O or 1 and l), then press Copy text and paste into your claim form or spreadsheet.

How it works

  1. Prepare: small pictures are enlarged (up to 3×) and very large ones reduced to 2,500 px, so letters are a size the engine reads well.
  2. Enhance (optional): the picture becomes black and white at the threshold that best separates text from page (Otsu's method); white text on a dark background is flipped.
  3. Recognise: Tesseract's LSTM neural network reads each line in the languages you ticked and gives a confidence for each word.
  4. Tidy: words split by a hyphen at the end of a line are joined, and lines inside a paragraph can be joined into one.

Tips for accurate text

  • Straight and sharp: hold the phone parallel to the page and tap to focus; blurry letters cause most errors.
  • Even light: avoid shadows across the text, or tick Enhance.
  • Only the right languages: extra languages slow it down and can add odd characters.
  • Printed text only: handwriting is not supported well.
  • Need the text shorter or reworded? Paste it into the AI Summarizer or Paraphrasing Tool.
