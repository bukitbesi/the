How the dates are worked out

  1. Due date = first day of the last period + 280 days + (cycle length − 28).
  2. Pregnancy week = days since the (adjusted) last period ÷ 7.
  3. Maternity leave: 98 consecutive days including rest days and public holidays; the first day counts as day 1.
  4. Allowance = ordinary daily rate of pay × 98 (at least RM6 a day).

Conditions to know

  • Maternity allowance: 90 days' work in the 9 months before confinement, with the employer in the 4 months before, and fewer than 5 surviving children; the leave is still given without allowance otherwise.
  • Confinement means a birth after at least 22 weeks; a miscarriage before that is not maternity leave.
  • Returning early within the 98 days needs a doctor's certificate and the employer's consent.
  • The Ministry of Health recommends at least 8 antenatal visits during pregnancy.
