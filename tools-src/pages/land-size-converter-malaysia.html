Malaysian land units explained

  • Relung (also relong): 30,976 sq ft, the traditional unit in Kedah, Perlis and Seberang Perai, still common for padi and kampung land. 1 relung = 484 jemba.
  • Jemba: 64 sq ft, the small unit inside a relung.
  • Orlong: 57,600 sq ft, found on old grants on Penang Island and in Melaka.
  • Acre, rood, pole: the imperial units on older titles. 1 acre = 4 roods = 160 poles, and 1 pole = 272.25 sq ft.
  • Hectare and square metre: the metric units on newer titles and in JUPEM survey plans. 1 hectare = 10,000 m².
