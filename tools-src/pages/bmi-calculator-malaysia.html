How to use your result

  1. BMI: weight in kg ÷ (height in m)². It screens for weight risk; it does not measure body fat directly.
  2. Waist: belly fat carries the most risk. Aim for under 90 cm (men) or 80 cm (women), and a waist under half your height.
  3. Target: losing 5% to 10% of body weight already lowers blood sugar and blood pressure for people who are overweight.
  4. Check-ups: adults with a BMI of 23 or more should have their blood pressure, blood sugar and cholesterol checked at a clinic.
