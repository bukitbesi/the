Hajj Cost Malaysia 2026 and 2027

The actual Muassasah hajj cost is RM33,300 a pilgrim for 2026 and 2027: B40 pilgrims pay RM15,000, M40 RM23,500 and T20 the full cost. To receive a hajj offer your Tabung Haji balance must reach RM15,000, and registered depositors must get there by 31 December 2028. Enter your balance and monthly saving.

How this calculator works

  1. Payment = your category's rate; assistance = RM33,300 − payment.
  2. Monthly minimum balance = the balance before that month's saving arrives.
  3. Hibah = rate × the average of 12 monthly minimum balances, credited every 12 months.
  4. Months to reach = the first month the balance reaches RM15,000 (or your hajj payment).

Hajj rules to know in 2026

  • Seruan Istito'ah (11 September 2026): anyone who has not performed hajj enters the queue automatically once their balance reaches RM15,000, replacing manual registration.
  • Offers: a RM15,000 balance and a health check are required; 2027 offer letters went to 31,600 pilgrims from 21 September 2026.
  • Age: no automatic priority at age 60 in the normal queue; age, mahram, health and average balance are weighed in appeals.
  • Private hajj packages (licensed hajj operators) cost much more and get no assistance; prices vary by company.
  • Zakat: TH pays zakat on savings for depositors. See also the zakat calculator.
