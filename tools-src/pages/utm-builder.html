      How to build a UTM link

      1. Paste the page address people should land on. Pasting a link that already has UTM tags fills the fields.
      2. Pick a preset or type a source, medium and campaign. Keep names short, lowercase and consistent.
      3. Check the GA4 channel. If it says Unassigned, change the medium.
      4. Copy the link into your post, bio, email or WhatsApp message, or download the QR code for print.
      5. See the results in GA4 under Reports, Acquisition, Traffic acquisition, by Session source / medium or Session campaign.

      How GA4 sorts UTM traffic into channels

      Google's default channel group checks the medium and source in a fixed order. These results come from the same rules the builder uses:

      {{UT_ROWS}}
      Source and medium, and the GA4 channel they land in
      utm_sourceutm_mediumGA4 channel

      The source lists for social, search, video and shopping sites come from a spreadsheet Google maintains; this builder includes the best-known ones (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Google, Bing, YouTube, Amazon). For other sources, a clear medium such as social or email decides the channel.
