Malaysian passport photo rules

  • Who needs to bring a photo: only children under 4 (a 35 × 50 mm print) and MyOnline Passport renewals (a 100–300 KB JPG). Applicants aged 4 and above are photographed at the immigration counter.
  • Background: white.
  • Clothing: dark coloured; a dark-coloured tudung that does not cover the forehead.
  • Not allowed: spectacles, coloured contact lenses and hats.
  • Face: facing forward, eyes open, mouth closed. Children under 4: face 25 to 30 mm.

Example: a MyOnline Passport renewal

Stand in front of a light wall near a window and have someone take the photo at eye level. Choose the MyOnline Passport size, keep the White background, and check the top of the head touches the first red line. Press Download JPG: the tool exports 827 × 1181 pixels and tunes the quality so the file lands between 100 and 300 KB, for example 180 KB, ready to upload.

How the tool works

  1. Remove the background: the MODNet AI model (Apache-2.0) makes a person mask in your browser.
  2. Find the head: the mask is scanned row by row from the top; the widest part is the head and the narrowest part below it the neck, so the chin lies between them.
  3. Size it: the photo is scaled so the head fills the official range (for example 25–30 mm of 50 mm) and centred.
  4. Export: 300 dpi for prints; for KB limits, the JPG quality is binary-searched until the file size fits.

Phone photo tips

  • Use even daylight from the front; avoid shadows behind the head.
  • Shoot from about 1.5 m and zoom in slightly so the face is not distorted.
  • Print the 4R sheet at a photo shop or kiosk as a normal photo print, then cut along the grey lines.
  • Just need a background removed for something else? Use Remove Background.
