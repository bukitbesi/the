How to edit a photo online

  1. Open a photo, drop it on the picture or paste it (Ctrl+V). For a design from scratch, choose New canvas.
  2. Crop and straighten under Crop: pick a ratio for Instagram, TikTok or YouTube, rotate or flip.
  3. Fix light and colour under Adjust, or tap Auto enhance; try a look under Filters. Hold "Hold to compare" to see the original.
  4. Add text, shapes or drawing. Each is a layer you can move, resize, rotate, fade or blend.
  5. Export as JPG for photos, PNG for graphics with sharp text, or WebP for websites.
