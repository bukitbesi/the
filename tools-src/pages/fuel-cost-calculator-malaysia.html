How fuel cost is calculated

  1. Litres needed = distance (km) × consumption (L/100 km) ÷ 100. If you know km per litre: distance ÷ km per litre.
  2. Fuel cost = litres × price per litre. Add tolls if any.
  3. Cost per km = consumption ÷ 100 × price per litre.
  4. Sharing = total ÷ number of people.
  5. BUDI95: litres in the month up to the quota at RM1.99, the rest at the unsubsidised RON95 price.

BUDI95 and BUDI Diesel

  • BUDI95: RON95 at RM1.99 a litre for Malaysians with a valid MyKad and driving licence, up to 300 litres a month from 1 September 2026 (200 litres from April to August 2026). E-hailing drivers can get an extra quota.
  • BUDI Diesel: subsidised diesel at {{F_DIESEL_BUDI}} a litre for eligible private diesel vehicle owners, 300 litres a month (400 litres for approved pick-ups and 4×4s).
  • Sabah, Sarawak and Labuan: diesel at {{F_DIESEL_EAST}} a litre.

Check eligibility and your remaining quota on the official BUDI MADANI portal or your petrol station app.

How to use less fuel

  • Cruise at 80–100 km/h on highways; consumption rises sharply above 110 km/h.
  • Keep tyres at the pressure on the door label; soft tyres raise consumption.
  • Remove extra weight and unused roof racks.
  • RON97 is only worth it if your owner's manual calls for it; most cars are designed for RON95.
