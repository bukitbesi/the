How RPGT works, step by step

  1. Net sale: sale price minus agent commission and legal fees.
  2. Total cost: purchase price plus legal fees, stamp duty and valuation, plus improvements that add value. For property bought before 2000, the market value on 1 January 2000 replaces the price.
  3. Gain: net sale minus total cost. A loss can be set against other gains.
  4. Exemption: individuals deduct RM10,000 or 10% of the gain, whichever is greater. A citizen or PR may instead exempt one private residence once in a lifetime.
  5. Tax: chargeable gain × the rate for the year of sale.
  6. Retention and filing: the buyer's lawyer pays 3% of the price to LHDN (5% for a company selling within 3 years, 7% for a non-citizen seller); under self-assessment you file CKHT 1A and pay any balance, and the buyer files CKHT 2A, within 60 days.
