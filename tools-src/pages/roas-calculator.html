How ROAS and ROI are worked out

  1. Ad spend = amount billed, plus 8% service tax if your invoice adds it.
  2. ROAS = sales from ads ÷ ad spend.
  3. Profit after ads = sales × gross margin − ad spend; ROI = profit ÷ ad spend.
  4. Break-even ROAS = 1 ÷ gross margin; ACoS = ad spend ÷ sales.
  5. CPA = ad spend ÷ orders; break-even CPA = average order value × gross margin.

Service tax on online ads in Malaysia

  • Rate: 8% service tax on imported digital services from 1 March 2024 (6% before).
  • Who charges it: registered foreign digital service providers charge it on their invoices; check whether your Meta, TikTok or Google invoice shows it.
  • Count it as spend: unless your business can recover it, the tax is part of the ad cost.
