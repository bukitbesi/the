How to convert units

  1. Choose a category: length, weight, volume, area, temperature, speed, time, data, pressure, energy, power or fuel economy.
  2. Type the value. Decimals and negative numbers (for temperature) work; commas are ignored.
  3. Pick the units to convert from and to, or press ⇄ to swap them. Every other unit appears in the table.
  4. Share or print: the page address keeps your conversion.

Malaysian traditional units

Malaysia went metric in the 1970s, but the kati and tahil are still used at wet markets, in gold and herb shops and in old recipes, and the gantang in older padi and rice records.

Definitions used

  • Length: 1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly; 1 foot = 12 in = 0.3048 m; 1 mile = 1.609344 km; 1 nautical mile = 1,852 m.
  • Mass: 1 pound = 0.45359237 kg exactly, so 1 kg = {{U_KG_LB}} lb; 1 ounce = 1/16 lb; 1 carat = 0.2 g.
  • Volume: 1 US gallon = 3.785411784 L; 1 imperial gallon = 4.54609 L; US cup = 1/16 US gallon (236.6 ml); the metric cup used in Malaysian and Australian recipes is 250 ml.
  • Area: 1 acre = 43,560 sq ft = {{U_ACRE_M2}} m²; 1 hectare = 10,000 m². For relung, orlong and old land titles use the land size converter.
  • Data: SI prefixes are powers of 1,000 (1 MB = 1,000,000 bytes); IEC binary prefixes are powers of 1,024 (1 MiB = 1,048,576 bytes); 1 byte = 8 bits.
  • Power: mechanical horsepower = 550 ft·lbf/s = {{U_HP}} kW; metric horsepower (PS, used by many car makers) = {{U_PS}} kW. An air conditioner rated 9,000 BTU/h moves {{U_1BTU9000}} W of heat; that is cooling capacity, not its electricity use.
  • Energy: 1 kWh (one TNB unit) = 3,600 kJ; the food Calorie is the kilocalorie, 1 kcal = 4.184 kJ.

Note: results are rounded to 10 significant figures for display. For legal trade, engineering or medical dosing, check the figure against the instrument or document that sets the requirement.
