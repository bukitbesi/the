Spin the Wheel: Random Name Picker

Enter names, one per line, and press Spin: the wheel picks a winner at random with equal chances, {{W_CHANCE}}% each for 8 names. Lucky draw mode picks several winners at once with no repeats. Cryptographic randomness, free, and your names are never sent anywhere.

      Example: drawing 3 winners from 8 names

      A class has 8 pupils: Aisyah, Ben, Chong, Devi, Farid, Grace, Hafiz and Imran. Choose Lucky draw, set 3 winners and press Spin. Each pupil has a {{W_CHANCE}}% chance of being the first winner and a 3 ÷ 8 = 37.5% chance of being among the 3. Nobody can win twice in one draw.

      If Farid bought three tickets, write Farid x3. The total weight becomes 10, so Farid's chance on a single spin rises to 3 ÷ 10 = 30% and every other name gets 10%.

      How the wheel picks a winner

      1. Read the list: each line becomes an entry; "x3", "*3" or "(3)" at the end gives weight 3. Blank lines and lines starting with # are skipped.
      2. Draw at random: a 32-bit number comes from crypto.getRandomValues. Numbers in the biased tail are rejected and redrawn, so each entry gets exactly its weight ÷ the total.
      3. Spin to the winner: the stopping angle is worked out from the winner's slice, plus 5 full turns and a random point inside the slice, and the wheel eases out to it.
      4. Lucky draw: winners are drawn one at a time and taken out of the pool, so there are no repeats.

      Ways to use it

      • Teachers: pick who answers next, split groups, or set presentation order.
      • Events and family days: door-gift lucky draws in full screen on a projector.
      • Social media: pick a giveaway winner from the commenters' names and screen-record the spin as proof.
      • Everyday: the What to eat? button chooses between nasi lemak, roti canai, laksa and more.
