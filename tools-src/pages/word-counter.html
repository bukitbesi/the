How to use the word counter

  1. Paste, type or open a file (.txt, .md or Word .docx). Counts update as you type.
  2. Set a goal if you need a minimum or target length, such as a 1,500-word article or a 250-word essay.
  3. Check readability and shorten long sentences if the score is below 60.
  4. Check keyword density to spot repeated words, and type your focus keyword to see if it appears early.
  5. Check the limits before posting to X, Instagram, LinkedIn or YouTube.

How keyword density is calculated

Density is the share of all words taken up by a word or phrase: times used × words in the phrase ÷ total words × 100. In a 500-word article, a two-word phrase used 5 times has a density of 2%. Phrases that start or end with a common word (the, and, yang, dan…) are skipped so the table shows topic words.
