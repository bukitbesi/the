GDEX Tracking

Enter your GDEX consignment note number to open GDEX's official tracking page with the number copied. Many GDEX numbers are MY followed by 11 digits. Below: what GDEX statuses mean and what to do if a delivery fails.

GDEX consignment note number

  • GDEX calls the tracking number a consignment note number, printed on the label (the "CN").
  • Many recent GDEX numbers are MY followed by 11 digits. The format is shared with other couriers' references, so the tracker marks it as a possible match; check the courier name on your receipt or order page.
  • GDEX's own tracking page accepts several consignment numbers at once, which helps when a seller sends an order in more than one box.

Common GDEX tracking statuses

StatusWhat it means
Picked upGDEX collected the parcel from the sender.
In transit / Outbound from stationThe parcel left a GDEX station for the next one.
Inbound at stationThe parcel arrived at a station, usually the one serving your area.
Out for deliveryA courier has the parcel for delivery.
DeliveredGDEX recorded the delivery, often with the receiver's name.
Undelivered / ReturnedDelivery failed or the parcel is going back to the sender.

GDEX's exact wording may differ; the order of events is what matters.

If a GDEX delivery fails

  1. Check the reason on the tracking page, such as nobody home or an incomplete address.
  2. Contact GDEX through its official website with the consignment number to rearrange delivery or collect from the station.
  3. For online orders, tell the seller too, so the order is not marked as completed.

About GDEX

GDEX (GD Express) was established in 1997. Its holding company, GD Express Carrier Bhd, listed on Bursa Malaysia's ACE Market in 2005 and moved to the Main Market in 2013. It delivers through a network of branches, agents and lodge-in centres across Malaysia.
