The overtime formulas

  1. Ordinary rate of pay (ORP): monthly wages ÷ 26 (section 60I). Daily-rated employees use their daily wage.
  2. Hourly rate of pay: ORP ÷ normal hours of work in a day.
  3. Normal day overtime: at least 1.5 × the hourly rate for each hour (section 60A).
  4. Rest day: up to half the normal hours, ½ day's ORP; over half, 1 day's ORP; beyond the normal hours, 2 × the hourly rate (section 60). Daily-rated: 1 or 2 days' wages.
  5. Public holiday: 2 days' ORP on top of holiday pay; beyond the normal hours, 3 × the hourly rate (section 60D).

Who gets these rates

Since 1 January 2023 the Employment Act protects every employee in Peninsular Malaysia and Labuan. The overtime, rest-day and public holiday rates in the First Schedule apply to:

  • employees earning up to RM4,000 a month, and
  • whatever they earn: manual workers, drivers and operators of mechanically propelled vehicles, supervisors of manual workers, and domestic employees.

Normal hours are capped at 8 a day and 45 a week, and overtime at 104 hours a month. Sabah and Sarawak have their own Labour Ordinances.
