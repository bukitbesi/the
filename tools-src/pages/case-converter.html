Title case rules used (AP style)

  • Capitalise nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, pronouns and any word of four letters or more.
  • Lowercase these words unless they come first, last or after a colon: {{CC_SMALL}}.
  • Hyphenated words follow the same rule for each part: Step-by-Step.

AP style and the Chicago Manual of Style differ on longer prepositions (Chicago lowercases with, from and about; AP capitalises them). The converter cannot tell when to, up or on is part of a verb, so check phrasal verbs such as "Set Up" by hand.

Developer naming conventions

Words are split at spaces, punctuation and lower-to-upper case changes, so user profile, userProfile, user_profile and User-Profile all convert the same way. Apostrophes are dropped (don't becomes dont). Letters with accents are kept in code cases and removed only in the URL slug.

Note: Malay headings follow their own capitalisation guidance; the Title Case button uses English AP rules, so check small words such as di, dan and yang in Malay titles by hand.
