How the comparison is worked out

  1. Now: each month, interest = balance × rate ÷ 12; your fixed payment, or the 5% / RM50 minimum, is taken off until the balance is zero.
  2. Loan: flat-rate instalment = (loan + loan × rate × years) ÷ months, or the reducing-balance (EIR) instalment.
  3. Loan cost = interest + 0.5% stamp duty.
  4. Saving = interest now − loan cost.

Credit card rates and rules in Malaysia

  • Finance charges: 15% a year (minimum paid on time 12 months in a row), 17% (10 of 12 months), 18% (everyone else).
  • Minimum payment: 5% of the balance or RM50, whichever is higher.
  • Cash advances: at most 18% a year, plus a withdrawal fee.
  • Personal loans: mostly advertised at flat rates; Bank Negara bans flat-rate pricing for new personal financing from 1 January 2027.

Before you consolidate

  • Compare the loan's EIR, not its flat rate, with your card rate.
  • Choose the shortest term you can afford: a long term lowers the instalment but adds interest.
  • Close the cards or cut their limits once paid off so the debt does not come back.
  • If you are struggling to pay, AKPK runs a free Debt Management Programme (DMP).
