How the ASB dividend is calculated

ASB units are fixed at RM1, so the rate in sen per unit is also the percentage return. ASNB works the distribution out in three steps:

  1. Take the lowest balance in each month from January to December.
  2. Add the twelve balances and divide by 12 to get the average monthly lowest balance.
  3. Multiply by the total rate (dividend + bonus). The amount is credited as new units around 1 January.

Because each month counts its lowest point, a withdrawal on the 30th wipes out that month's earnings on the money withdrawn, and a deposit on the 2nd waits a month to count. Save on the 1st and withdraw, if you must, on the 1st of the next month.

ASB financing: when it pays

With ASB financing, a bank lends you the money to buy units up front and holds the certificate as security. You pay interest on a falling loan balance, but earn the distribution on the full amount every year. It pays while the ASB rate stays above the break-even rate the calculator shows. The financing rate usually floats with the bank's base rate, and some banks require takaful (MRTT) cover, which adds to the cost.
